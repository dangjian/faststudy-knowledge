## Part A

### Let's try

Which month are they talking about? Listen and tick.

March. / April. / September. / February.

### Let's talk

Mr Jones: There are some other special days in May.

Zhang Peng: What are they?

Mr Jones: The school art show and the reading festival.

Zhang Peng: When is the art show?

Mr Jones: It's on May 1st.

Zhang Peng: It's Labour Day. When is the reading festival?

Mr Jones: It's on May 5th.

Zhang Peng: Wow! May is fun!

（配图：日历——May 1st art show；May 5th reading festival）

Talk about the holidays with your partner.

When is China's National Day?

It's on October 1st.

（配图：节日日历——Children's Day（June 1st）、Labour Day（May 1st）、China's National Day（October 1st）、New Year's Day（January 1st））

### Let's learn

（配图：四月活动公告——April 1st swimming contest；April 2nd maths test；April 3rd Chinese test；April 4th English test；April 5th sports meet）

When is the sports meet?

### Ask and answer

（配图：便签——Jul. 1st singing contest；Dec. 4th maths test；Mar. 5th school trip；Sept. 2nd Miss Chen's birthday；Nov. 2nd English test）

Read, listen and chant.

（配图：th 浊音词卡——this（这）、mother（妈妈）、that（那）、brother（哥哥））

2. ______（Thursday/Monday is his eleventh/third/fourth birthday.）
