## Part B

### Let's try

Listen and circle.

（配图：标志——Quiet, please! / No eating!）

### Let's talk

Tom: My name is Tom. What's your name?

John: Shh. Talk quietly. I'm John. I can show you the English books.

Tom: Thanks.

John: Here they are.

Tom: OK. Can I read the books here?

John: Yes. Of course.

Tom: Anything else?

John: Yes. Keep your desk clean.

Tom: OK, I will. Thanks.

Match, say and act.

（配图：提示语卡片——Work quietly! / Talk quietly! / Keep your desk clean!）

Oh, my English book is here!!!

### Let's learn

（配图：文明守则图片——Talk quietly. / Keep to the right. / Keep your desk clean. / Work quietly.）

Can we use your crayons?

OK. Take turns. Talk quietly, please.

### Look, match and say

1. He is talking in the library. —— A. Keep to the right.

2. We are playing a game. —— B. Talk quietly.

3. She is colouring at her desk. —— C. Take turns.

4. They are writing in class. —— D. Keep your desk clean.

5. They are walking on a bridge. —— E. Work quietly.

He is talking in the library.

Talk quietly.

### Read and write

The world robot exhibition

（配图：世界机器人展览，各国机器人（Spain 等））

Ni hao. Ni zai gan shen me ne?

Oh, hello. What are you doing?

I'm drawing a picture. Have a look. It's lovely.

What are you saying?

I'm speaking Chinese. Sorry.

Are you cooking?

Yes. I'm cooking rice. Asako is making sushi.

Can we play music with you?

Sure. Please take turns.

Are you from China?

Yes, I am.

Are you doing kung fu?

Yes, I am. I'll show you.

You're so cool! Can you teach me?

Sure!

Fill in the blanks.

1. The Canadian robot is ______ a picture.

2. Asako is ______ sushi.

3. The Spanish robot is ______ music.

4. Robin is ______ kung fu.

5. Sarah and Robin are ______ to the Spanish robot.

### Let's check

Listen and tick.

Listen again and write.

2. What is Chen Jie doing?

### Let's wrap it up

Look and write.

1. It's 7:00 a.m. We ______ at 7 o'clock.

2. I sometimes clean my bike on the weekend. I ______ it now.

3. It's 9 p.m. They ______ now, but they often go to bed at 10 p.m.
