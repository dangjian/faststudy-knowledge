# Unit 3 My school calendar

## 单元主页

（配图：小熊问）When is the summer vacation?

（配图：校历公告板（上半年）——January (Jan.) New Year's Day；February (Feb.) winter vacation；March (Mar.) Tree Planting Day；April (Apr.) sports meet；May Labour Day，Mother's Day；June (Jun.) Children's Day，Father's Day）

When is Tree Planting Day?

It's in March.

When is Dragon Boat Festival?

It's usually in June. My mum will make zongzi then. I hope you can come and share some with us.

That's very kind of you.

（配图：校历公告板（下半年）——It's in July and August. July (Jul.) summer vacation；August (Aug.) summer vacation；September (Sept.) Teachers' Day，Mid-Autumn Festival；October (Oct.) China's National Day；November (Nov.) American Thanksgiving Day；December (Dec.) Christmas）

My school calendar

When is the school trip this year?

It's in May.

Is the singing contest in May, too?

Yes.

Hooray for May!
