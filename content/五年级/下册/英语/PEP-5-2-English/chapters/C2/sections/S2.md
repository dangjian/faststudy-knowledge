## Part B

### Let's try

Zhang Peng and John are looking at some pictures. Which is Zhang Peng's picture? Listen and tick.

### Let's talk

Amy: Hello, Miss White. Look at my picture.

Miss White: Good job! I like the trees. The colours are very pretty!

Amy: Yes. I like autumn best. The weather is good and the colours are beautiful! Which season do you like best, Miss White?

Miss White: Summer.

Amy: Why?

Miss White: Because I like summer vacation!

Ask and answer.

Which season do you like best?

I like spring best.

Why?

Because there are pretty flowers.

### Let's learn

（配图：活动图片——go on a picnic、go swimming、pick apples、make a snowman、play in the snow、plant flowers、eat ice cream）

Which season do you like best?

I like spring best. There are beautiful flowers everywhere. I often go on a picnic with my family.

Ask and answer.

Which season do you like best?

Winter. I often play in the snow.

### Read and write

Robin likes them all!

Look at the green trees and pink flowers. I like spring because there are beautiful flowers everywhere.

The weather is hot, hot, hot! I like summer, but I can't swim.

What lovely colours! The leaves fall and fall and fall. I love fall! Wow! I want to paint a picture, too!

There is lots of snow. It is white everywhere. I like winter because I can play in the snow.

Read and tick.

1. The last "fall" means ______.

autumn，cute，pretty，cool

2. Which season does Robin like?

Spring. / Autumn. / Summer. / All the four seasons.

Which season do you like best? Why? Complete the sentence.

I like ______ best because ______.

### Let's check

Listen and write.

Girl: ______

Boy: Do you like summer?

Girl: No, I don't. It's too hot.

Boy: ______

Girl: ______ The colours are beautiful. I can pick fresh apples.

### Let's wrap it up

Fill in the question words.

（what，when，where，which，why）

1. ______ do you like spring best?

Because the weather is nice.

2. ______ season do you like best?

I like spring best.

3. ______ do you go to school?

I go to school at 8 o'clock.

4. ______ do you often do on the weekend?

I often go shopping.

5. ______ is your photo?

It's on the wall.

### Story time

Zip & Zoom: Welcome!

Koala: Thank you. Merry Christmas!

Zip & Zoom: Merry Christmas!

Zoom: Which season do you like best?

Koala: Summer. I like sunny days.

Zoom: What do you usually do on Christmas Day?

Koala: I usually go to the beach and swim in the sea!

Zoom: You swim in winter?!

Koala: Christmas is in summer in Australia, so we never have snow for Christmas.

Zoom: Let's make a snowman.

Koala: Wow! A white Christmas!

Zip & Koala: Great!

Zip: Say "cheese"!

Koala: Cheese!
