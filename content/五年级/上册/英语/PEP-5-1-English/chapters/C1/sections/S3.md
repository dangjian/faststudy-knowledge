## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

**Join us for the school talent show!**

（配图：魔术师变出兔子）Everyone can draw pictures. But can you make your pictures come to life? Mr Zhang will draw a lovely rabbit, and it will come to life in front of your eyes!

（配图：John 和 Amy 跳绳）Jumping rope is easy and fun. Meet the jumping stars: John and Amy. They are wonderful jumpers!

（配图：Binbin 弹钢琴和琵琶）Binbin loves music. He can play the piano and the pipa! Come and listen!

（配图：机器人 Robin 下中国象棋）Robin is a clever robot. He is good at playing Chinese chess. Can you play Chinese chess? Come and play with him!

Read and complete the mind map. Then introduce the performers.

（配图：思维导图，中心词 Performers，分支：The Magic Teacher——make pictures come to life；The Jumping Stars；Super Binbin；Robin the Robot）

Add a performer to the talent show and write about him/her.

### Project: Make an album of your friends

1. The children are telling Miss White about their friends. Listen and tick or cross.

（配图：四幅图，孩子们在下象棋、画画、打篮球、拉二胡）

2. Listen again. Write and say.

1. Liu Jia is ______ and ______. She can ______.
2. Oliver is ______ and ______. He can ______.
3. Zhang Peng is ______. He is good at ______.
4. Li Xia is ______ and ______. She can ______.

（配图：对话气泡）

- What's Liu Jia like?
- She's ...
- What can she do?
- She can ...
- Is she good at ...?
- Yes, she is.

**Make an album of your friends.**

Step 1　Fill in the table.

| Name | What's he/she like? | What can he/she do? | What's he/she good at? |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Liu Jia | clever | play Chinese chess, jump rope |  |

Step 2　Choose some photos of your friends and write about them.

（配图：示例相册页）This is my friend Liu Jia. She can play Chinese chess. She is clever and often helps me. She can also jump rope well.

Share your album in class.

This is my friend Liu Jia. She's ...

### Self-check

1. I can tell others what my friends are like.
2. I can tell others what my friends and I can do.
3. I can use a mind map to talk about my friend's talent.
4. I can write about why someone is different.
5. I can write a sentence using words with "ai" or "ay".

### Reading time

（配图：Grace 来到新班级，同学们互相介绍）

We have a new student in our class today. Her name's Grace.
Class, this is Grace.
Hi, everyone. Nice to meet you all!
Grace is so lovely and friendly.
Yes. I like her.
Is she strong? Can she play football?
I don't know.
But she can't play football with us. What a pity!

（配图：足球比赛场景，Grace 画海报为球队加油）

Hey, everyone! Our football match is next week.
Great! We need a poster for the match.
Can you draw, Grace?
Yes, I can draw really well.
GO! GO! GO!
What a goal!
Hooray!
We won! Hooray!
Thank you for your help, Grace.
You're welcome.
Yes. The poster is great!
