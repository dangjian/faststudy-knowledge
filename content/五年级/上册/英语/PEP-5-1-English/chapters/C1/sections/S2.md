## Part B What can you do?

### Let's talk

Miss White and Mrs Green are talking about a school festival. Listen and choose.

1. Miss White and Mrs Green like ______.
   A. science　B. music　C. sports
2. Mrs Green can ______.
   A. play the erhu　B. play the piano　C. play football

Binbin: The Science and Art Festival is in two weeks.
Sarah: Yes. What can you do for the festival?
Binbin: I can make a robot.
Oliver: Oh! I like making robots too. We can work together.
Binbin: Great! Can you make robots, Sarah?
Sarah: No, I can't.
Oliver: What can you do?
