## Unit 4

- How can we all stay healthy? —— 我们怎样才能保持健康？
- We should eat healthy food. —— 我们应该吃健康的食物。
- Can I play on Mum's phone? —— 我可以玩玩妈妈的手机吗？
- No. You shouldn't play on a phone so often. —— 不行。你不应总是玩手机。

## Unit 5

- What would you like to eat? —— 你想吃些什么？
- I'd like beef and rice. —— 我想吃牛肉和米饭。
- Would you like some chicken soup too? —— 你还想喝鸡汤吗？
- Yes, please. —— 是的，请（给我鸡汤）。
- Where does your coconut come from? —— 你的椰子是哪里的？
- Hainan! —— 海南！

## Unit 6

- There are also many rivers in the nature park. —— 这个自然公园还有许多河流。
- Is there a waterfall in the forest? —— 森林里有瀑布吗？
- Yes, there is. —— 是的，这里有。
- Are there any famous mountains in the nature park? —— 自然公园有名山吗？
- Yes, there are. —— 是的，这里有。
- There will be heavy rain in the afternoon too. —— 今天下午还将有大雨。
