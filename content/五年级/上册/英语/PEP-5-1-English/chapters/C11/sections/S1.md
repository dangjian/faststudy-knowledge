## Unit 1

- What's he like? —— 他什么样？
- He's tall and strong. —— 他又高又壮。
- Is he good at football? —— 他擅长踢足球吗？
- Yes, I think he is. —— 是的，我想他擅长。

## Unit 2

- Hi, Mike. You look sad. What's the matter? —— 嗨，迈克。你看起来不开心。怎么回事？
- It's about our school project. —— 是关于我们学校的项目。
- And we have to finish our project by Monday. —— 并且我们得在星期一之前完成项目。
- So you don't agree with each other? —— 所以你俩意见不一致？
- Why not do both? —— 为什么不两个活动都做呢？
- It's important to listen to each other. —— 听取彼此的想法很重要。
- Yes, Miss White. I need to talk to Mike. —— 是的，怀特老师。我需要和迈克谈谈。

## Unit 3

- What's your favourite day at school, John? —— 你在学校最喜欢哪一天，约翰？
- What do you have on Wednesdays? —— 星期三你有什么课？
- I have Chinese, maths, music and PE. —— 我有语文课、数学课、音乐课和体育课。
- What do you usually do at the weekend, children? —— 孩子们，周末你们通常做什么？
- I often play football. Sometimes I play Chinese chess with my father. —— 我经常踢足球。有时我和爸爸下象棋。
- Does your mother work on both Saturdays and Sundays? —— 你妈妈周六周日都工作吗？
- Yes, she does. —— 是的，她（周末两天）都工作。
