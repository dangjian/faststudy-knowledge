# Revision New Year's party

## Read aloud and act it out

（配图：New Year's party 场景）

Miss White: Children, New Year's Day is coming. Let's have a class party. What can you do for the party?
Zhang Peng: I can draw pictures.
Binbin: I can choose the music for the party.
Amy: Let's play the piano together, John.
John: Great idea!
Miss White: Wonderful!
Chen Jie: Oliver and I are in the Cooking Club. We can make some food for the party.
Mike: Sarah, Liu Jia and I are in the Drama Club. We can put on a play.
Binbin: I want to tell a story.
Miss White: It's time for English class. Let's talk about it later.

## Read and write

Dear students,

Our New Year's party is next Monday. I can see you are all very excited. I am too. I have some tips for you:

1. Plan your time well.
You need to prepare for the party. There will also be tests on Wednesday and Friday. You are very hard-working, but don't forget to rest.

2. Stay healthy.
It is flu season. You should get enough sleep. You should also eat healthy food and drink enough water.

3. Don't be afraid to ask for help.
If you have any problems, don't worry. Everyone has problems. You can always talk to your parents, and you can ask me for help too.

Yours,
Miss White
