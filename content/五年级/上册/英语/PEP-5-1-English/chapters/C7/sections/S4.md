## Play Bingo

1. Read and group the words.

beef, book, autumn, day, coat, draw, goat, know, lie, night, talk, tea, play, right, rain, slow, see, too

2. Choose 16 words from above and write them in the boxes below. Write a different word in each box.

3. Listen and cross out the words you hear. Four in a row? Say "Bingo!"
