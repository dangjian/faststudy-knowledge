## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Do you have healthy habits?

Name: David

1. What do you usually eat?
I like hamburgers and ice cream. I eat them every day. I don't often eat green vegetables.

2. Do you usually do sports?
Yes. I have PE every day from Monday to Friday, but I don't do sports at the weekend.

3. How many hours do you sleep every day?
I usually sleep about ten hours a day, but sometimes I only sleep six hours.

4. How do you usually spend your free time?
I like watching TV, but I don't spend too much time on it.

5. Do you often play with your friends?
Yes. I play with my friends every day. We often play football together.

Read and answer: what are David's habits?

Good habits: ______

Bad habits: ______

Complete the tips below and share in class.

How can you live a healthy life?

You should ... / You shouldn't ... (so often)

### Reading time

（配图：How to look after your eyes，护眼指南）

Healthy life

How to look after your eyes

- You should eat healthy food, like green vegetables. Eating fish is good too.
- Give your eyes a break. You shouldn't watch TV or check your phone so often.
- You should exercise outside every day.
- You should get enough sleep. Sleep rests your eyes.

（配图：How to look after your teeth，护齿指南）

Healthy habits

How to look after your teeth

- Brush your teeth at least twice a day, in the morning and in the evening. It keeps your teeth healthy.
- Brush your teeth for more than two minutes.
- Eat healthy snacks like nuts.
- Eat less candy.
- Don't drink too many sweet drinks.
