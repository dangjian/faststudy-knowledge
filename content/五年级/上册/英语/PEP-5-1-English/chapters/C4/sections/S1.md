## Part A How can we stay healthy?

### Let's talk

Mike's mother and Miss White are talking on the phone. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. Mike has a cold. (　)
2. Mike should see a doctor. (　)

Miss White: Mike has a cold today, children. He has to stay at home.
Binbin: Oh no! It's flu season now.
Miss White: That's right. How can we all stay healthy?
Amy: We should eat healthy food.
Miss White: Good. And what else?
Zhang Peng: We should exercise every day.
Miss White: Yes, but please drink enough water.
Chen Jie: We should also get enough sleep, Miss White.
Miss White: Right. And if you don't feel well, you should see a doctor.

### Look, match and say

（配图：连线配对图，小朋友说"No breakfast today. I'm late."）

You should ...

- eat breakfast.
- eat healthy food.
- exercise every day.
- get enough sleep.
- wash your hands.

### Let's learn

（配图：Stay healthy 健康习惯图）

Eat healthy food.

Drink enough water.

Exercise every day.

Get enough sleep.

- How can I stay healthy?
- You should get enough sleep. It's a good habit.

### Look, write and role-play

（配图：Mike 与老师对话场景）

- Eat healthy food.
- Hello, Ms Wang!
- Hi, Mike.
- What healthy habits do you have?

### Let's spell

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

ow / oa

We put on our coats and play in the snow.
Then slowly walk to feed the goats.
We wash our hands with soap.
Good habits are our goal, you know.

**Read and say. Then listen and circle.**

1. get / goat / got
2. go / goal / grow
3. ow / slow / snow
4. red / road / rod

**Listen, write and say.**

1. There is ______ on the ______.
2. I put on my ______.
3. The ______ is ______ in the ______.

Write a sentence using some words with oa or ow.
