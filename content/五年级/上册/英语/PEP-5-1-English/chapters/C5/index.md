## Part B Where does our food come from?

### Let's talk

Mrs Green is talking with the children. Listen and choose.

1. What are they talking about?
   A. Drinks.　B. Food.　C. People.
2. Which drink comes from Yunnan?
   A. Water.　B. Tea.　C. Apple juice.

Mrs Green: What's your favourite fruit, Binbin?
Binbin: Coconuts are my favourite.
Mrs Green: Why do you like coconuts?
Binbin: Because coconut water is nice. And it's healthy.
Mrs Green: We can also cook delicious meals with coconut.
Binbin: Yes! And some people make houses with coconut tree leaves.
Mrs Green: That's interesting. Where does your coconut come from?
Binbin: Hainan! There are many coconut trees there.

### Look, ask and answer

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

**The lotus plant**

Lotus flowers are very beautiful. They have many colours. Lotus plants grow in lakes around the world. It is also a great plant to eat.

Lotus seeds are small and round. We can cook and eat them in different ways.

Lotus leaves are big, round and green. We can eat the young leaves. We can also cook rice in the leaves.

We eat lotus roots. The big brown and yellow roots are delicious. There are many ways to cook them.

### Reading time

（配图：The history of tea，茶的传播史，含植物结构图 flower, seeds, leaves, stem, root）

**The history of tea**

At first, people didn't drink tea. But they did eat tea leaves!

Later, more people began to drink tea. There were many different kinds of tea. People put tea leaves in hot water to make a drink.

- How can I make a good cup of tea?
- Water is very important.

Tea was first popular in China. Then it travelled to Japan and other countries. In the 1700s, tea became popular in the UK.

In China, some people like to cook chicken and eggs with tea leaves. They make cakes and ice cream with tea too!

Now, tea is popular all over the world. There is green tea, black tea, flower tea ... There really is a tea for everyone!

- What would you like to eat?
- Where does your tea come from, Zip?
- Most of my tea comes from China!
