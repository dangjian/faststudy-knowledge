## Part A What do we eat?

### Let's talk

Oliver is at home now. Listen and tick.

1. How is Oliver feeling now?
2. What would Oliver like to drink?

Oliver: Mum, I'm hungry.
Mum: What would you like to eat?
Oliver: I'd like beef and rice.
Mum: No problem. Would you like some chicken soup too?
Oliver: Yes, please.
Mum: You should also have some salad. Vegetables are good for you.
Oliver: OK. Can I have some ice cream, please?
Mum: Yes, but just a little.
Oliver: OK. Thanks, Mum.

### Look, write and say

（配图：点餐场景）

My order / ______'s order

- What would you like to eat?
- I'd like ...
- What would you like to drink?
- Can I have some ...?

### Let's spell

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

oo

Look! Look! I can cook!
Look at my cool cookbook.
The cookbook has good food,
And it looks cool too.

**Read and say. Then listen and group.**

book, foot, too, good, tool, cook, cool

**Listen, write and say.**

1. I want to ______.
2. I'd like to look at the ______.
3. This ______ is really ______.

Write a sentence using some words with oo.
