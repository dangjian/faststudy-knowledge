# Unit 2 My feelings

## Part A How do we express our feelings?

### Let's talk

John is talking with his mum. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. They are talking about John's school project. (　)
2. John wants to go to the old people's home. (　)

Chen Jie: Hi, Mike. You look sad. What's the matter?
Mike: It's about our school project. John wants to go to the children's centre, but I want to clean up the park.
Chen Jie: I see. So you don't agree with each other?
Mike: That's right. And we have to finish our project by Monday. I'm so worried!
Chen Jie: Why not do both? I can help. We can work together.
Mike: That's a good idea!

### Choose and talk

- My mother is sick.
- I can't find my hat.
- It is raining. I can't play football.
- My brother doesn't agree with me.

Chen Jie: He's excited.

### Let's spell

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

ee / ea

A sheep meets a friendly bee.
It is so happy to see the bee.
The sheep and the bee speak over tea.
Oh! The summer season is so free!

**Read and say. Then listen and tick.**

1. see / sheep
2. eat / meet
3. tea / team
4. bee / free

**Listen, write and say.**

1. How do bees ______ flowers?
2. What do sheep ______?
3. Can animals think and ______?

Write a sentence using some words with ee or ea.

## Part B How can we understand each other better?

### Let's talk

Binbin sees Mike. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. Binbin wants to clean up the park. (　)
2. Mike and Binbin have different ideas. (　)

Miss White: What's the matter, John? You look worried.
John: It's about our project. Mike and I want to do different things.
Miss White: I see. It's important to listen to each other.
John: Yes, Miss White. I need to talk to Mike. But I'm afraid he's angry with me.
Miss White: Look, here he comes. You can talk to him now.
Mike: John, I'm sorry. Let's do both projects! Chen Jie can work with us.
John: That's great!

### Choose and talk

| Problem | Advice |
|---------|--------|
| I am not good at ... | You can talk to ... |
| We don't agree with each other. | You need to listen to ... |
| ... is angry with me. | Let's ... |

（配图：对话气泡）

- What's the matter, Oliver? You look sad.
- I want to sing, but I'm not good at it.
- You can talk to ...
- Good idea! Thanks.

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Dear Mike,

I am happy at school. I have many friends. I play with them in the playground, and we share ideas in class. We laugh and study together all day. I enjoy school.

But my family just moved to a new home. I have no friends there. I feel sad. I don't want to tell my parents. I don't want them to feel bad.

What can I do? Can you help me?

Yours,
Jim

Read and fill in the table.

| Place | Feeling | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| At school | Jim is happy. |  |
| At home |  |  |

What can Jim do? Read and complete Mike's reply.

Dear Jim,

Don't feel sad. You can ______. You can also ______.

It is OK to feel bad sometimes. It is always good to share your feelings.

Yours,
Mike

### Project: Make a feelings book

1. How are the children feeling? Listen, number and write.

1. ______ can't find his dog. He is ______.
2. ______ works hard and gets an A. She is ______.
3. ______'s parents don't agree with him. He is ______.

2. Talk to Mike, Amy or John.

（配图：建议气泡）

- It's important to share ideas.
- You need to listen to your parents.
- Don't worry. Let's look for it.
- Share our good news with friends.

（配图：对话气泡）

- I can't find my dog.
- Don't worry. Let's look for it.

**Make your feelings book.**

What makes me angry?

- Someone says bad things about me.
- Someone doesn't use kind words.

What do I want to do?

（配图：表情选项）shout, cry, run away

What can I do?

（配图：做法选项）listen to music, count to ten, talk to someone

Share your book in class.
