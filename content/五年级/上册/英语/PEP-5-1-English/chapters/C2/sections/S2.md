## Part B How can we understand each other better?

### Let's talk

Binbin sees Mike. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. Binbin wants to clean up the park. (　)
2. Mike and Binbin have different ideas. (　)

Miss White: What's the matter, John? You look worried.
John: It's about our project. Mike and I want to do different things.
Miss White: I see. It's important to listen to each other.
John: Yes, Miss White. I need to talk to Mike. But I'm afraid he's angry with me.
Miss White: Look, here he comes. You can talk to him now.
Mike: John, I'm sorry. Let's do both projects! Chen Jie can work with us.
John: That's great!

### Choose and talk

| Problem | Advice |
|---------|--------|
| I am not good at ... | You can talk to ... |
| We don't agree with each other. | You need to listen to ... |
| ... is angry with me. | Let's ... |
