## Part A What do you learn at school?

### Let's talk

It is Monday night. John is talking with his grandpa. Listen and tick the classes you hear.

Grandpa: What's your favourite day at school, John?
John: Wednesday.
Grandpa: What do you have on Wednesdays?
John: I have Chinese, maths, music and PE.
Grandpa: Maths is not easy.
John: No, but I really like my maths teacher!
Grandpa: That's good. Is music your favourite subject?
John: Yes. I really like singing. And I like PE too.
Grandpa: Great! Wednesday is your favourite day. But I like Tuesdays and Fridays.
John: Why, Grandpa?
Grandpa: Because I have my taijiquan class!

### Let's spell

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

ie / igh

One night, a baker makes some pies.
He doesn't lie down and works till light.
But he eats all his pies, and his belt is too tight.
It's not right to work and eat all night.

**Read and say. Then listen and tick.**

1. high / right
2. lie / light
3. pie / tie
4. light / night

**Listen, write and say.**

1. I like eating ______.
2. I eat them every ______.
3. It's not ______ to eat too many pies.

## Part B What do you do at the weekend?

### Let's talk

Miss White and Mr Li are talking. Listen and fill in the blanks.

1. They are talking about ______ activities.
2. ______ often cooks. ______ usually goes to the park with his dog.

Miss White: What do you usually do at the weekend, children?
Zhang Peng: I often play football. Sometimes I play Chinese chess with my father.
Miss White: That's nice.
Sarah: My mother works at the weekend, so I often cook lunch for my family.
Miss White: Good girl! Does your mother work on both Saturdays and Sundays?
Sarah: Yes, she does.
Chen Jie: I do my homework on Saturdays. And I usually have pipa lessons on Sundays.
Miss White: Great! Have a nice weekend, everyone!

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Miss White asks the students what they do after school. Here are the answers from Li Xia and Eric.

Dear Miss White,

I play ping-pong with Binbin on Mondays and Thursdays. On Tuesdays, I cook dinner because my mother works on Tuesday nights. On Wednesdays and Fridays, I usually read books. At the weekend, I often play with my friends in the park or visit my grandparents. I am busy, but I always do my homework on time!

Li Xia

Dear Miss White,

On Monday, Tuesday and Friday evenings, I play football. I often watch TV on Wednesdays and Thursdays. On Saturdays and Sundays, I play basketball and go running. I am so busy. I don't have much time to study.

Eric

Dear parents,

At our school we want our students to find a good work-play balance. Please read our school's top five tips.

1. Make a good schedule. Remember to have both work and play.
