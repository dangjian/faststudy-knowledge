## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Miss White asks the students what they do after school. Here are the answers from Li Xia and Eric.

Dear Miss White,

I play ping-pong with Binbin on Mondays and Thursdays. On Tuesdays, I cook dinner because my mother works on Tuesday nights. On Wednesdays and Fridays, I usually read books. At the weekend, I often play with my friends in the park or visit my grandparents. I am busy, but I always do my homework on time!

Li Xia

Dear Miss White,

On Monday, Tuesday and Friday evenings, I play football. I often watch TV on Wednesdays and Thursdays. On Saturdays and Sundays, I play basketball and go running. I am so busy. I don't have much time to study.

Eric

Match Miss White's comments to the answers above.

（配图：两条评语）Please try to find good work-play balance. / You balance your work and play very well.

### Project: Make a chart about work-play balance

1. What does Ron do on different days? Listen and write.

Ron's week

1. ______ is Ron's favourite day. He has ______, English, ______ and PE.
2. Ron usually visits his grandparents on ______.
3. Ron does his homework on Sunday mornings. He ______ and ______ in the afternoon.
4. Ron doesn't ______ at the weekend.

2. What do you do on different days? Talk and write.

My favourite weekday and the subjects I have

My weekend activities

- What's your favourite weekday?
- It's ...

### Self-check

1. I can talk about the classes I have each day.
2. I can tell others about my weekend activities.
3. I can give examples of good and bad work-play balance.
4. I can plan for my after-school activities.
5. I can write a sentence using words with "ie" or "igh".

### Reading time

（配图：校长写给家长的一封信，配五条建议插图）

Dear parents,

At our school we want our students to find a good work-play balance. Please read our school's top five tips.

1. Make a good schedule. Remember to have both work and play.

（配图：一周日程表 Sun. Mon. Tue. Wed. Thur. Fri. Sat.）

2. Put tasks in order and take breaks. Don't do everything at the same time.

（配图：任务清单 Tasks）

3. Balance is important. There is a time for work and a time for play.

4. Stay healthy. Exercise, eat well and get enough sleep.

（配图：健康 Health 图标）

5. Ask for help. Don't stay quiet. All of our teachers want to help.

（配图：学生向老师求助）I need your help, Miss ...

Parents, please help us. We want all children to have a good work-play balance. Make sure they follow these five tips. It's important for us to work together.

Sincerely,
Liu Yang (School principal)

（配图：结尾小对话）

- Does Ms Liu help the students?
- Yes, she does. Look!
- Here's my to-do list.
- Wow!
