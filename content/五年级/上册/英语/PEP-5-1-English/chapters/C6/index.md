# Unit 6 Nature and us

（配图：单元主题图，自然风光）Why should we respect nature?

## 单元热身（Unit Opener）

**Think, show and tell**

Think of a place that you want to visit. Show a picture of that place and talk about it. Let other students guess the place.

**Look and think**

## Part A What is exciting about nature?

### Let's talk

Mike's family are in a nature park. They are talking with Mr Liu, the tour guide. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. It is a cloudy day. (　)
2. There are many animals in the nature park. (　)

Mike's dad: Wow! That lake is really lovely!
Mr Liu: Yes. There are also many rivers in the nature park.
Mike's dad: Is there a waterfall in the forest?
Mr Liu: Yes, there is. You'll see a beautiful one today!
Mike: Great! Are there any famous mountains in the nature park?
Mr Liu: Yes, there are. We'll visit one tomorrow. There are many flowers too. And there are rabbits!
Mike: That's great!

## Part B What happens in bad weather?

### Let's talk

Mike and his dad are talking about the weather. Listen and circle.

1. Today's weather is good / bad.
2. There will be rain / snow this afternoon.

Mr Liu: I'm sorry. The wind is very strong outside. We can't go on our trip this morning.
Mike's mum: I agree. Strong winds are often dangerous.
Mike's dad: There will be heavy rain in the afternoon too. Mike, stay in the hotel with Mum and close the windows. I'll go to buy some umbrellas and raincoats.
Mike's mum: There is a small shop in the hotel. You can buy them there.
Mike's dad: OK.
Mr Liu: Please don't worry! It'll be a fine day tomorrow.

### Listen, write and say

**Weekend plan**

|-----|---------|-------------------------|

| Sunday | There will be ... | I need to ... |

- I usually go to the park. But this Saturday there will be strong winds.
- I'll ...

Autumn is windy. Be careful of fires.

Sometimes there is heavy snow in winter. It is very cold.

Mike: Is there any bad weather here in summer?
Mr Liu: Yes. There is heavy rain. Sometimes there are floods.

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

**Things you should know before mountain hiking**

Hiking in the mountains can be a lot of fun, but there are some things you should know:

Wear good hiking shoes and the right clothes. It is very important to wear the right shoes. It is often windy in the mountains too, so bring a jacket with you.

Be careful of bad weather. Don't go hiking in bad weather! In summer there is heavy rain and in winter there is heavy snow.

Bring enough water. In the mountains there is often little clean water, so bring enough water with you.

Tell people about your trip. Tell your parents and friends before you go. They need to know where you are.

Read and complete the tips.

What should people take with them?

1. Wear good ______ and bring a ______.
2. Bring enough ______ with you on the trip.

What should/shouldn't people do?

1. Check the weather before you go. Don't ______ in bad weather.
2. Tell ______ before you go.

Write three tips for going to the sea in hot weather.

1. The sun is strong. You should wear ______.
2. You should also wear ______ on your trip. And bring ______ with you.
3. Be careful of ______. Please make sure you stay safe.

### Project: Write a welcome letter for a nature park

1. The children are talking about parks. Listen and number the pictures.

（配图：自然公园图片，含"注意防火"标识）

2. Read and introduce these nature parks to your class.

**Welcome to River Nature Park**

There are many rivers and big trees here. There are a lot of fish in the rivers. You can go boating on the river with your friends. There is heavy rain in summer. Sometimes there are floods too. Check the weather report before you go.

**Mountain Nature Park**

There are many mountains, and there is a big forest. It is a great place to hike or walk your dog. There is often heavy fog on spring mornings. Don't go into the forest then. In autumn, it is often windy. Be careful of fires.

**Welcome to Waterfall Nature Park**

There is a big waterfall here. You can take pictures of your family near the waterfall. It is very cold in winter. Sometimes there is heavy snow. Check the weather report before you go.

### Reading time

（配图：三江源国家公园风光）

**Sanjiangyuan National Park**

Sanjiangyuan National Park is in Qinghai. It is the largest national park in China. Do you think you can see it all in a week?

There are so many beautiful views. You can see snowy mountains, lakes, rivers, forests, grasslands and much more. Some people say you need a month!

There are also three great rivers. The Yangtze River, the Yellow River and the Lancang River all start here. This place gives fresh water to millions of people in China. It is sometimes called the "Water Tower of China"!

The park is a gift from nature. There are many birds, fish and other animals. Sanjiangyuan National Park really is a magical place! There is so much to see!

- Yes, there are. And there will be more in the future!
