# 第3单元 UNIT 3 Same or Different?

**BIG Question:** How do we compare with each other?

In this unit, you will

1. compare people and things.
2. discuss whether friends should be similar or different.
3. talk about how you are different from the past.
4. discover why differences are important.

Look and share

1. What is the possible relationship between the girls in the photo?
2. What do they have in common?
3. How are they different from each other?

**Grammar Focus**

**3a** Read the sentences and notice the comparatives in bold. Find more examples in Section A.

There are **more** activities this year. It is **more colourful** than last year.
Emma is **taller** than Ella, but her hair is **shorter** than Ella's.
Ella dances **better** than Emma, but Emma sings **louder** than her.
Emma always gets up **earlier** than me, but I work as hard as her!

**3b** Use the words in brackets to write questions and answers.

1. Q: *Is Julie slimmer than you?* (Julie / slim / you)
   A: No, she isn't. She is as *slim* as me.
2. Q: ______? (you / serious / your cousin)
   A: No, I'm not. I am ______ than he is.
3. Q: ______? (Tom / smart / Sam)
   A: No, I don't think so. I think Sam is as ______ as Tom.
4. Q: ______? (Jack / swim / fast / Sally)
   A: No, he doesn't. He swims as ______ as Sally.
