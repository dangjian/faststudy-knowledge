## Part A Who lives with you?

### Let's talk

（配图：Chen Jie 带 Sarah 回家见家人的场景）

Chen Jie: Mum! Dad! This is my friend, Sarah Miller.

Mum & Dad: Hi, Sarah.

Sarah Miller: Nice to meet you.

Chen Jie: This is my grandma. This is my grandpa.

**Listen, sing and play**

（配图：家庭成员合影场景）

### Let's learn

（配图：家庭成员图片，grandmother (grandma)、grandfather (grandpa)、mother (mum)、father (dad)、sister、me）

Chen Jie: Hi, I'm Chen Jie. Look! This is my family. This is my ...

**Listen and chant**

This is my mum.
This is my grandma.
Hello, hello, hello!
Nice to meet you.
This is my dad.
This is my grandpa.
Hi, hi, hi!
How do you do?
This is my sister.
How are you?

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Ee　egg　nest

Ff　fish　beef

Gg　girl　pig

Hh　hot　hat

（配图：四幅小图，egg nest、fish beef、girl pig、hot hat）

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

b e d → b e d

bed　fed　gab　had

**Write and say.**
