# Unit 2 Different families

（配图：单元主题图，一家人在四合院里挂灯笼、贴春联）What makes a family?

## 单元热身（Unit Opener）

**Look and think**

Why are the people together? How are families different?

**Listen and chant**

We are a family
A happy family,
My father, my mother,
My sister and me!

**Listen and sing**

（配图：歌曲页 My family）

## Part B How are families different?

### Let's talk

（配图：Chen Jie 和 Sarah 看家庭照片的场景）

Chen Jie: Sarah, you have a big family. Is this your sister?

Sarah Miller: No, it's my cousin.

Chen Jie: Is that your brother?

Sarah Miller: Yes, it is.

**Ask and answer**

Is this your brother?

No, it's my cousin.

### Let's learn

（配图：家庭成员图片，uncle、aunt、cousin、baby sister、brother）

My family is big. I have a brother, a baby sister, a cousin ...

**Listen and chant**

I have an uncle.
I have an aunt.
I have two cousins too.
I have a brother,
And a baby sister.
They can play with me.

### Start to read

（配图：两张全家福对比，小家庭和大家庭）

This is my mum.
And this is my dad.
This is my family.
This family is small.
They love each other.

This family is big.
They love each other too.

**Tick Chen Jie's family members.**

aunt　baby sister　brother　cousin　father　grandfather　grandmother　mother　sister　uncle

**Draw your family. Ask and answer in pairs.**

Is this your brother?

No, it's my ...

### Self-check

1. I can introduce my family to others.
2. I can name different family members.
3. I can say how families are different.
4. I can read, write and say Ee, Ff, Gg and Hh.

### Reading time
