**Listen and chant.**

**Being a good guest**

Wave your hands and look in the eyes.
Say "Hi!" or say "OK" to be polite.
Be a good guest. Be polite!
We are nice. We play fair.
Share your toys and listen with care.
Be a good guest. Show we care!

## Read the list aloud.

How can we be a good guest?

- Ask Mum and Dad. (Can I go?)
- Knock! (Knock! Knock! Say "Hello!")
- Say "Thank you!" (Thank you!)
- Care and share. (Let's share!)
