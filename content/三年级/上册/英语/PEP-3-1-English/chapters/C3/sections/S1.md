## Part A What pets do you know?

### Let's talk

（配图：Wu Binbin 到 Mike 家做客，看到 Mike 的狗）

Wu Binbin: Good morning, Mike!

Mike: Good morning! Come in.

Wu Binbin: I like your dog.

Mike: Thanks. Do you have a pet?

Wu Binbin: No, I don't.

Chen Jie: Yes, I do. I have a cat.

### Let's learn

（配图：宠物店 Pet Shop 场景，fish、cat、bird、rabbit、dog）

I like fish.
I like cats.

**Listen and do**

Run, run, run like a dog.
Sleep, sleep, sleep like a cat.
