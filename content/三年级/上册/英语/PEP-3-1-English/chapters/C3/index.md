## Part B What wild animals do you know?

### Let's talk

（配图：Miss White 带学生们去动物园的场景）

Miss White: Let's go to the zoo!

Students: Great!

Student: Look, Miss White. What's this?

Miss White: It's a fox.

Student: What's that?

Miss White: It's a red panda.

Mike Black: It's cute!

**Play with hand shadows**

（配图：手影游戏场景）

What's that?

It's a fox.

### Let's learn

（配图：动物园场景，monkey、panda、tiger、elephant、lion）

It's a monkey.
It's a panda.
It's a tiger.
It's an elephant.
It's a lion.

**Listen and chant**

What's this? It's an elephant.
What's this? It's a monkey.
What's this? It's a panda.
What's this? It's a lion!
What's this? It's a tiger!
Run!

### Project: Make an animal picture book

**1.** What animals do the children like? Listen and circle.

**2.** Are these animals different? Think and group them.

bird　dog　elephant　fish　cat　giraffe　lion　monkey　rabbit　panda　tiger

| Pets | Wild animals |
|------|--------------|
| cat | panda |

**3.** Choose one group from activity 2.

**4.** Make your book.

### Self-check

1. I can tell others about my pets.
2. I can talk about wild animals.
3. I can group different animals.
4. I can read, write and say Ii, Jj, Kk and Ll.

### Reading time

（配图：大海里，小蓝鲸和鲸鱼妈妈）

Baby whale: Mum, what makes us great?

Mother Whale: We are big.

Baby whale: Elephants are big too.

Mother Whale: We are fast.

Baby whale: Fish are fast too.

Mother Whale: We eat a lot.

Baby whale: Pandas eat a lot too.

Baby whale: I know! We sing!

Mother Whale: Yes! We can sing nice songs.
