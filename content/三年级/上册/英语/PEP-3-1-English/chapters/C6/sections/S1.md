## Part A When do we use numbers?

### Let's talk

（配图：Mike 向 Wu Binbin 介绍弟弟 Sam，Xinxin 也来问好）

Mike: Binbin, this is my brother, Sam.

Wu Binbin: Hi, Sam! This is my sister, Xinxin.

Sam: Hello, Xinxin!

Xinxin: Hi, Sam!

Sam: How old are you?

Xinxin: I'm five years old.

Sam: Me too.

**Choose and role-play**

（配图：大(1)班的名牌，Feifei、Xiaoli）

Hello, Xiaoli! How old are you?

I'm five years old.

### Let's learn

（配图：数字图片，one、two、three、four、five）

**Listen and chant**

Jump! Jump! Jump! One, two, three!
Jump! Jump! Jump! Three, two, one!
Four and five! Four and five!
Jump up high! Jump up high!
Five! Four! Three, two, one!
Jump! Jump! It's such fun!

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Vv　van　vet

Ww　we　win

Xx　box　six

Yy　yellow　yo-yo

Zz　Zip　quiz

（配图：五幅小图，van、we、box、yellow、quiz）

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

wet　vat　won　yes　yum

**Listen and circle the last sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词尾字母的练习）

**Write and say.**
