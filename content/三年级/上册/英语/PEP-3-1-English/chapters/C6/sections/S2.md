## Part B How useful are numbers?

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们数水果、商量去商店买文具）

Student A: How many apples?

Student B: Two.

Student A: How many bananas?

Student B: Three. And one orange.

Student A: OK. I have ten yuan.

Student B: I have six yuan.

Student A: Great! Let's go to the shop!

**Count and say**

How many lions?

Two.

### Let's learn

（配图：文具商店付款场景，标价 ¥6、¥7、¥8、¥9、¥10）

Shop assistant: That's ten yuan, please.

Customer: Here you are.

six　seven　eight　nine　ten

**Listen and chant**

One head, two legs, ten toes.
I am a child.
One head, eight eyes, eight legs.
I am a spider.
One head, four wings, six legs.
I am a bee.

### Start to read

（配图：连环画，Sam 过生日切蛋糕）

It's seven o'clock. Hurry!

One orange, two apples and three bananas.

Happy birthday!

Dogs don't eat cake, Sam!

Three cuts. Let's eat!

Oh, one more cut for the dog.

**Circle the numbers you read in the text.**

one　two　three　four　five　six　seven　eight　nine　ten

**Say the numbers in each group.**

（配图：把数字按 odd（奇数）和 even（偶数）分组，示例：five、one、seven / four、six）

### Project: Make a birthday card

**1.** It's Xinxin's birthday soon. Listen and circle.

1. How many people are there?
   - Three.
   - Four.
2. How old is Matt?
   - Five.
   - Six.

**2.** Read the card and circle the numbers.

（配图：生日卡片，HAPPY BIRTHDAY，5 p.m.，8/1，Xinxin's home）

1. How old is Xinxin now?
   - Five.
   - Seven.
2. What time is the party?
   - 5 p.m.
   - 6 p.m.
3. What numbers do you see on the card?
   - One.
   - Two.
   - Five.
   - Seven.
   - Nine.
   - Six.
   - Eight.
   - Ten.

**3.** Make your birthday card.

（配图：手工生日卡片，Matt）

**4.** Give your birthday card to a friend.

Happy birthday!

Thanks!

### Self-check

1. I can ask about age and number.
2. I can count things with the numbers 1 to 10.
3. I can use the numbers 1 to 10 in different places.
4. I can read, write and say Vv, Ww, Xx, Yy and Zz.

### Reading time

（配图：Zoom 和 Zip 玩数字卡片游戏）

Zoom: How many cards?

Zip: Six.

Zoom: What's this?

Zip: Six.

Zoom: Right.

Zoom: What's this?

Zip: Hmm, six?

Zoom: You're right again.

Zoom: What's this?

Zip: It's six in Chinese.

Zoom: What about this?

Zip: I don't know.

Zoom: This is six too. It's a Jiaguwen number. Jiaguwen is over 3,000 years old!

Zip: Wow!
