# Unit 5 The colourful world

（配图：单元主题图，色彩斑斓的海底世界，两条黄色的蝴蝶鱼）Why are colours important?

## 单元热身（Unit Opener）

**Look and think**

What colours do you see? What colours do you like?

**Listen and chant**

The sun is orange.
Grass is green.
What about the sky and sea?
Roses are red.
Snow is white.
Please, please tell me why!

**Listen and sing**

（配图：歌曲页 Colour song）

## Part A What colours do you see?

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们玩颜色混合实验的场景）

Student A: What colour is it?

Student B: It's orange.

Student A: What colour is it?

Student B: It's green.

Student A: Look! Red and blue make purple.

Student B: Wow!

**Guess and check**

（配图：颜色混合猜测游戏）

What colour is it?

It's green.

Wow! Blue and yellow make green.

### Let's learn

（配图：The world 主题图，a yellow duck、a brown bear、a purple flower、green grass、blue sea）

a yellow duck

a brown bear

a purple flower

green grass

blue sea

**Listen and chant**

I see colours here and there.
Purple flowers, green grass and blue sea.
I see colours here and there.
A big brown bear. What else can you see?

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Qq　quiet　queen

Rr　red　ruler

Ss　see　bus

Tt　Ted　sit

Uu　up　run

## Part B How do colours help us?

### Let's talk

（配图：两个小朋友一起画画的场景）

Student A: What colours do you like?

Student B: I like red and pink.

Student A: OK. Let's draw some red and pink flowers.

Student B: And the birds?

Student A: Let's draw some purple and brown birds.

**Colour and say**

What colours do you like?

I like green and yellow.

### Let's learn

（配图：颜色卡片，red、pink、orange、white、black）

Colours are useful!

**Listen and do**

Black, black, sit down.
White, white, turn around.
Pink and red, touch the ground.
Orange and red, jump up and down.

### Start to read

（配图：四种颜色标识的场景，Colours can talk!）

**Colours can talk!**

It's red. Red can say "No!"

It's green. Green can say "Go!"

It's blue. Blue can say "Use again!"

It's yellow. Yellow can say "Be careful!"

**Circle the colours you see in the pictures.**

black　blue　brown　green　orange　purple　white　red　pink　yellow

**Read, think and colour.**

（配图：调色练习，blue + yellow，____ + red）

What colour is it?

### Project: Make a colour flip book

**1.** Which colours do the children say? Listen and circle.

**2.** Which colours match the things? Talk and add colour labels.

（配图：示例，green——What colour is it? It's green.）

black　blue　brown　orange　pink　red　white　purple　yellow

**3.** Make your book. Choose the colours you like.

Friend: What colours do you like, Sarah?

Sarah: I like yellow, red and blue.

（配图：Step 1: Choose the colours you like，Step 2: Make your book）

**Share your book in class.**

This is my book. I like yellow, red and blue. Bananas are yellow. Ducks are ...

### Self-check

1. I can tell others about the colours I see and like.
2. I can tell the colour of things.
3. I can talk about what colours mean.
4. I can read, write and say Qq, Rr, Ss, Tt and Uu.

### Reading time

（配图：向日葵生长过程）

**I am a sunflower**

I am a sunflower. I am tall and green.

Then I am green and yellow.

Now I am a big yellow flower. Bees come. They are yellow and black. I give them food.

Now it is cold. I am green and brown.

I am gone. But my children grow.
