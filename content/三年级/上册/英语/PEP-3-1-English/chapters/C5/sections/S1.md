## Part A What colours do you see?

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们玩颜色混合实验的场景）

Student A: What colour is it?

Student B: It's orange.

Student A: What colour is it?

Student B: It's green.

Student A: Look! Red and blue make purple.

Student B: Wow!

**Guess and check**

（配图：颜色混合猜测游戏）

What colour is it?

It's green.

Wow! Blue and yellow make green.

### Let's learn

（配图：The world 主题图，a yellow duck、a brown bear、a purple flower、green grass、blue sea）

a yellow duck

a brown bear

a purple flower

green grass

blue sea

**Listen and chant**

I see colours here and there.
Purple flowers, green grass and blue sea.
I see colours here and there.
A big brown bear. What else can you see?

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Qq　quiet　queen

Rr　red　ruler

Ss　see　bus

Tt　Ted　sit

Uu　up　run

（配图：五幅小图，queen、ruler、bus、Ted、up）

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

quit　rot　set　tap　tub

**Write and say.**
