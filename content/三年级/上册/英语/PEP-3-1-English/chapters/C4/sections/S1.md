## Part A What do we get from plants?

### Let's talk

（配图：Miss White 和 Mike 在农场里的场景）

Miss White: Mike, do you like apples?

Mike: Yes, I do. And you?

Miss White: No, I don't. I like bananas.

Miss White: Do you like the farm?

Mike: Yes, Miss White. I like the fresh air.

**Ask and answer**

Do you like apples?

Yes, I do.

### Let's learn

（配图：水果图片，bananas、grapes、oranges、apples）

Do you like apples?

No, I don't. I like bananas.

**Listen and chant**

Grapes are small. Bananas are long.
Apples and oranges make you strong.
Trees grow and give us things:
Fresh air, flowers and leaves in spring.

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Mm　map　mum

Nn　new　fan

Oo　orange　fox

Pp　pen　cup

（配图：四幅小图，map mum、new fan、orange fox、pen cup）

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

n o d → n o d

nod　hop　men　nap　pig

**Write and say.**

Mm　Nn　Oo　Pp
