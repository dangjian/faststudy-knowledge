## Part B How can we help plants?

### Let's talk

（配图：绿色校园海报，同学们讨论养护校园花园）

Student: The school gardens need help.

Student: We can water the flowers.

Teacher: Nice. And?

Student: We can water the grass.

Student: We can plant new trees.

**Look and talk**

（配图：绿水青山从我做起海报）

How can we help?

We can plant trees.

### Let's learn

（配图：植物生长要素图片，sun、air、trees、water、flowers、grass）

Plants need our help.

Plants need air, water and sun.

**Listen and chant**

Air, water and sun.
These can all help plants grow.
Plant, water, cut and turn.
These are some things I know.

### Start to read

（配图：Help 与 Give 主题图，孩子们给小树浇水，果树给大家果实）

Plants can give us many things. We need plants.
Plants need air, water and sun. Can we help them?

**What fruit do you see? What does this fruit tree need?**

**Circle the things you see in the picture. Then talk about a plant.**

apples　bananas　grapes　oranges　flowers　air　water　sun　grass　tree

Flowers need air, water and sun. We can help them. We can ...

### Project: Make a paper garden

**1.** Which fruit do the children like? Listen and match.

（配图：Binbin、John、Chen Jie、Sarah 与水果连线）

**2.** What do you know about fruit trees? Talk in your group.

What do fruit trees need?

How can we help?

What can they give us?

A. air　B. apples　C. bananas　D. grapes　E. oranges　F. plant the tree　G. sun　H. water　I. water the tree

（示例：A, G）

**3.** Make your paper garden.

（配图：制作步骤，Step 1: Make some cards，Step 2: Make your garden（sun、apple tree））

**Share your paper garden in class.**

This is my garden. The apple trees need air, water and sun. We can help them. We ...

### Self-check

1. I can ask about fruit and say the fruit I like.
2. I can name some plants and talk about how they grow.
3. I can say how we help plants and plants help us.
4. I can read, write and say Mm, Nn, Oo and Pp.

### Reading time

（配图：小苹果树与新家庭的故事）

**I am an apple tree**

I am an apple tree. My new family help me.

I need air, water and sun. My family water me.

It is cold. They help me.

My family like me. I love the fresh air.

The flowers are so beautiful!

I am big now. I give apples to my family. We are all happy!
