## Part A How do we greet friends?

### Let's talk

（配图：Mike 与 Wu Binbin 见面问好的场景）

Mike Black: Hello!

Wu Binbin: Hi! My name is Wu Binbin.

Mike Black: I'm Mike Black.

Wu Binbin: Nice to meet you.

Mike Black: Nice to meet you too.

**Role-play**

Sarah Miller: Hello! My name is Sarah.

John Baker: Hi! I'm John.

（配图：人物头像，John Baker、Wu Binbin、Chen Jie、Sarah Miller、Mike Black、(your name)）

### Let's learn

（配图：Sarah 与 Mike 互相介绍的场景）

Mike Black: Hello! My name is Mike Black.

Sarah Miller: Hi! I'm Sarah Miller.

（配图：身体部位图片，eye、ear、mouth、hand、arm）

**Listen and do**

Wave your hand. Hello!

Look into my eyes. Hi!

Point to your ear. Listen!

Point to your mouth. Smile!

Wave your arm. Bye!

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Aa　apple　bag

Bb　bed　Bob

Cc　cat　can

Dd　dog　sad

（配图：四幅小图，apple bag、bed Bob、cat can、dog sad）

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

d a b → d a b

bad　cab　dad

**Write and say.**
