# Unit 1 Making friends

（配图：单元主题图，孩子们背着书包上学，一个女孩向同伴伸出手打招呼）How do we make friends?

## 单元热身（Unit Opener）

**Look and think**

Are they friends? What makes you a good friend?

**Listen and chant**

Hello! Hello!
What's your name?
Nice to meet you.
Let's play a game!

**Listen and sing**

（配图：歌曲页 Nice to meet you!）

---

## Part A How do we greet friends?

### Let's talk

（配图：Mike 与 Wu Binbin 见面问好的场景）

Mike Black: Hello!

Wu Binbin: Hi! My name is Wu Binbin.

Mike Black: I'm Mike Black.

Wu Binbin: Nice to meet you.

Mike Black: Nice to meet you too.

**Role-play**

Sarah Miller: Hello! My name is Sarah.

John Baker: Hi! I'm John.

（配图：人物头像，John Baker、Wu Binbin、Chen Jie、Sarah Miller、Mike Black、(your name)）

### Let's learn

（配图：Sarah 与 Mike 互相介绍的场景）

Sarah Miller: Hello! My name is Sarah Miller.

Mike Black: Hi! I'm Mike Black.

（配图：身体部位图片，eye、ear、mouth、hand、arm）

**Listen and do**

Wave your hand. Hello!

Look into my eyes. Hi!

Point to your ear. Listen!

Point to your mouth. Smile!

Wave your arm. Bye!

### Letters and sounds

**Listen, repeat and chant.**

Aa　apple

Bb　bag

Cc　cat

Dd　bed

（配图：四幅小图，apple、bag、cat、bed）

bad　can　sad　Bob　bop

**Listen and circle the first sound.**

（配图：听音圈出单词首字母的练习）

**Can you read the words?**

bad　cab　dad

**Write and say.**

Bb　Aa　Dd

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh Ii Jj Kk Ll Mm Nn Oo Pp Qq Rr Ss Tt Uu Vv Ww Xx Yy Zz

## Part B How can we be a good friend?

### Let's talk

（配图：Chen Jie 的书掉在地上，Sarah 帮忙捡起并分享文具的场景）

Sarah Miller: Oh no!

Chen Jie: It's OK, Chen Jie.

Sarah Miller: We can share.

Chen Jie: Thanks, Sarah.

（配图：Chen Jie 把铅笔递给 Sarah）

Chen Jie: Hey, Sarah! We can share.

Sarah Miller: Thank you, Chen Jie.

**Look and match**

Nice to meet you.　　Nice to meet you too.

We can share.　　Thank you.

Oh no!　　It's OK.

### Let's learn

（配图：四格图片，I smile. / I listen. / I help. / I share.）

I smile.

I listen.

I help.

I share.

**Listen and chant**

Am I a good friend? Yes, I am!
I listen and say "Hi!" I smile too.
Am I a good friend? Yes, I am!
I help and share. I play fair too.

### Start to read

（配图：海报，I am a good friend!）

I am a good friend!
I say "Hi!"
I listen.
I share.
I help.
I am nice to my friends.

**Circle the words you read in the poster.**

hand　arm　ear　eye　mouth　help　listen　share　say　smile

**Are you a good friend? Look and tick.**

（配图：判断好朋友行为的勾选练习）

### Project: Make a mind map of making friends

**1.** How can John make friends at school? Listen and circle.

**2.** What do you do with your friends? Talk in your group.

**3.** What do friends do?

（配图：思维导图素材卡片，Play together. / Listen. / Smile. / I say "Hi!" / I help. / I say "Bye!" / I share. / I smile. / I listen.）

**4.** Use activity 2 to make your mind map.

（配图：Make friends 思维导图示例）

**Share your ideas in class.**

Friends listen. Friends help and share ...

### Self-check

1. I can greet people and show friendliness.
2. I can say the names of body parts.
3. I can say different ways to be a good friend.
4. I can read, write and say Aa, Bb, Cc and Dd.

### Reading time

（配图：Zip 与 Zoom 见面问好）

**Zoom is my new friend**

Zip: Hi! I'm Zip.

Zoom: Hi! My name is Zoom.

Zip: Nice to meet you.

Zoom: Nice to meet you too.

（配图：Zoom 和 Zip 一起分享食物）

Zoom is nice. We share food.

We play together.

We listen with care and help each other.

（配图：Zip 摔倒了，Zoom 伸出手扶她）

Zip: Ouch!

Zoom: Hold my hand.

Zip: My mouth!

We are good friends now.
