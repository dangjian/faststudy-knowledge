# Appendix 2 Chants in "Letters and sounds" 语音板块歌谣

## Unit 1

1.
A, A, A. Show me an A!
B, B, B. Show me a B!
C, C, C. Show me a C!
D, D, D. Show me a D!
After me. A, B, C, D!
After me. A, B, C, D!

2.
A is for /æ/.
/æ/, /æ/, apple.
/æ/, /æ/, bag.
I see an apple in a bag.

B is for /b/.
/b/, /b/, bed.
/b/, /b/, Bob.
I see Bob in the bed.

C is for /k/.
/k/, /k/, cat.
/k/, /k/, can.
I see a cat and a can.

D is for /d/.
/d/, /d/, dog.
/d/, /d/, sad.
The dog is sad.

## Unit 2

F, F, F. Show me an F!
G, G, G. Show me a G!
H, H, H. Show me an H!

Say E, F, G and H!

2.

/e/, /e/, egg.
/e/, /e/, nest.
I see an egg in the nest.

F is for /f/.
/f/, /f/, fish.
/f/, /f/, beef.

/g/, /g/, girl.
/g/, /g/, pig.

/h/, /h/, hot.
/h/, /h/, hat.
