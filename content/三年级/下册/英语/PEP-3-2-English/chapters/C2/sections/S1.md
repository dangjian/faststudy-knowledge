## Part A

### Look and think

- What do the children say to their grandma?
- How do you show your love to your family?

### Listen and chant

You are happy.
Jump and dance.
You are happy.
Smile and sing.
Love makes you happy,
And love is everything!

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Expressing yourself

### Let's talk

Your dog is cute. It has a long body and short legs.

Yes, that's right.

Do you like dogs?

Yes, I do. Dogs are friendly. They can help us too.

### Describe and choose

（配图：描述与选择图）

It's orange. It has a long tail and big ears.

It's the fox.

### Let's learn

I love my rabbit. It has long ears and a short tail.

（配图：形容词词汇图）long　fat　thin　slow　short

### Listen and chant

I have a cat.
He's old and fat,
And he's also very slow.
His tail is long.
His legs are short.
But I love him, don't you know?

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

He likes to run.
She likes to jump.
We sing and dance.
Be happy and have fun!

Read, listen and number.

he　　be　　she　　me　　we

Listen, circle and write.
