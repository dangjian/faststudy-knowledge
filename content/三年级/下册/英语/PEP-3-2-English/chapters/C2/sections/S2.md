## Part B

### Let's talk

Bye, Dad.

Bye, Mum.

Love you!

Do you often say that to your mum?

Yes, I do. What about you?

No, I don't. I often make her gifts.

That's nice.

### Do a survey

How do you show your love to your parents?

Do you often do this, John?

No, I don't.

（配图：调查表，John 一栏示例）

### Let's learn

Mum, we love you!

（配图：动作词汇图）draw a picture　make a card

Let's make a card.

（配图：动作词汇图）sing and dance

Let's sing and dance.

### Listen and chant

Make a card.
Draw a picture.
Make your mum smile.
Sing a song.
Dance together.
We all like to smile.

### Read and write

Read and number the pictures.

We express ourselves in many ways.

1. We talk. Our mouth, face and body can all "talk".
2. We draw pictures or make cards.
3. We sing songs or dance.
4. Smile! A smile can say so much!

（配图：四幅情景图，按句子顺序标号）

Look, choose and write.

I love my dog. It has a ______ body and ______ legs.

词库：big　long　short　slow　fat　thin

I often make a card for my dad. What about you?

I ______.

词库：dance　draw a picture　make a card　sing

## Project: Make mind maps of how we show friendship

Amy and her friend are talking about pets. Listen and tick.

（配图：听音勾选图——宠物图片）

Think about your pets or friends. Choose some words and talk in pairs.

Do you have a pet?

Yes, I have a dog. It's thin. It has a long body. I like it.

I have a pet friend.

1. big　2. fast　3. fat　4. long　5. short　6. slow　7. small　8. tall　9. thin

Do you listen to your friend with care?

Yes, I do.

Do you often play together?

I am a good friend.

1. draw a picture　2. listen with care　3. make a birthday card
4. play together　5. share my toys　6. sing and dance

Use activity 2 to help make your mind maps.

（配图：思维导图示例——中心为 "Ways to show friendship"，分支句型：It is ... / I have ... / It has ... / We often ... together）

Share your mind maps in class.

... is my friend. We share. We often draw pictures together.

I have a dog. It's ... It has ... We often play together.

### Self-assessment

1. I can describe my pet.
2. I can say how I show my love to my family.
3. I can describe ways people show feelings.
4. I can read and spell words with "-e".

## Reading time

（配图：水果创意漫画——用水果做各种造型）

Be creative! Have fun!

We like fruit. We can have fun with fruit.

We can make animals with fruit.

It has a long body.

It has a big mouth.

It has short legs.

We have friends. We can make fruit friends too.

We dance. We talk. We sing.

We can act as fruit. We can be tall or short. We can be fat or thin.

We can also "write" with fruit.

We can express our ideas in different ways!

It is fun!
