# Appendix 4 Useful expressions

常用表达法

## Unit 1

What's your name?
你叫什么名字？

My name's Amy Green.
我叫埃米·格林。

Where are you from?
你是哪里人？

I'm from the UK.
我是英国人。

Let me help.
我来帮忙。

After you!
您先请！

You're welcome.
别客气。

Who's that girl?
那个女孩是谁？

That's our new neighbour, Amy.
是我们的新邻居埃米。

## Unit 2

It has a long body and short legs.
它有长长的身体和短短的腿。

Dogs are friendly.
狗是友善的。

Do you often say that to your mum?
你经常对妈妈那么说吗？

Yes, I do.
是的。

I often make her gifts.
我经常给她做礼物。

We express ourselves in many ways.
我们用很多种方式表达自己。

## Unit 3

Excuse me.
劳驾。

Can I use your eraser, please?
我能用一下你的橡皮吗？

Sure. Here you are.
当然。给你。

No problem.
没问题。

What are these?
这些是什么？

They're grapes.
是葡萄。

What about this?
这个呢？

## Unit 4

I'd like some bread and eggs, please.
我想吃点儿面包和鸡蛋。

Have some milk too.
也喝点儿牛奶吧。

Would you like some rice and meat?
要吃点儿米饭和肉吗？

Yes, please.
好的，谢谢。

Eat some every day!
每天吃一些吧！

## Unit 5

Do you have old things?
你有旧东西吗？

Yes, I do. I have some old books.
是的。我有些旧书。

This boat is cool.
这只船很酷。

You can keep it.
你可以留着它。

Mum, where is my animal book?
妈妈，我的动物书在哪儿？

Is it on the shelf?
在架子上吗？

No, it isn't.
不，不在。

It's in the box.
它在盒子里。

## Unit 6

How many books do we have?
我们有多少本书？

We have fifteen books.
我们有十五本书。

How many boxes do we need?
我们需要多少个箱子？

We need three boxes.
我们需要三个箱子。

How much is this bag?
这个包多少钱？

It's twenty yuan.
二十元。

Six yuan, or three for seventeen yuan.
六元，或者十七元三本。

How about three for sixteen yuan?
十六元三本行吗？
