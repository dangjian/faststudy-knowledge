语音

teacher　uncle　family
today　giraffe　banana

apple　about　elephant　computer

## 语调

（配图：语调示例图）

Listen with care.

Is that your sister?

What colour do you like?

Listen and read aloud. Notice the intonation.

1. A: Do you have a pet?
   B: No, I don't.

2. A: What's your name?
   B: My name's Amy Green.

Read, circle and match. Then listen and check.

- Do you have a pet?　——　Yes, I have a dog.
- Can I use your ruler, please?　——　Sure.
- Where is my cap?　——　It's in a box.
- How many books do you have?　——　I have five.
