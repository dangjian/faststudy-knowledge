# Unit 6 Numbers in life

How can numbers help us?

（配图：单元主题图——水果摊价签 ¥8.00、¥4.00、¥13.16、¥12.15（每 500g））

---

## Part A

### Look and think

- How many green apples do you see?
- When do you use numbers?

### Listen and chant

Numbers go up.
Numbers go down.
We sort, solve,
Check and count.
In the classroom,
And all around.

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Let me count!

### Let's talk

How many books do we have?

We have fifteen books.

How many pencils do we have?

Twelve.

How many boxes do we need?

We need three boxes.

### Count and say

（配图：数数练习图——Toys）

How many apples do we need?

We need fifteen.

How many apples do we have now?

Five.

### Let's learn

What do you do with old things?

（配图：调查统计图——Students：A = Give away，B = Sell，C = Take to school）

（配图：数字词汇图）eleven 11　twelve 12　thirteen 13　fourteen 14　fifteen 15

Fifteen students choose A. Twelve ...

### Listen and chant

Five and six makes eleven.
Add one, it's twelve.
Six and seven makes thirteen.
Add one, it's fourteen.
Three fives are fifteen.
We can count to fifteen!

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read the story aloud.

My cat has a big face.
Look at its legs. Cool!
The girl can't tell its age.
It's eighteen. Yes, my cat is so old.

Read, listen and tick.

1. cake　face
2. girl　orange
3. cool　long
4. my　yellow

Listen, circle and write.

## Part B

### Let's talk

How much is this bag?

It's twenty yuan.

How much is this book?

Six yuan, or three for seventeen yuan.

How about three for sixteen yuan?

OK.

（配图：情景图——Flea Market 跳蚤市场）

### Role-play

How much is this toy car?

It's twelve yuan.

### Let's learn

How much is this boat?

It's twenty yuan.

（配图：数字词汇图与时钟（16:00））sixteen　seventeen　eighteen　nineteen　twenty

### Listen and chant

One minute, tick.
Nineteen minutes, tick.
Two minutes, tock.
Twenty minutes, tock.
Sixteen, seventeen, eighteen. Time goes slowly,
Don't watch the clock.
When you watch the clock!

### Read and write

Read and tick.

（配图：短文配图——Save and Spend 存钱与花钱，Flea Market 跳蚤市场）

One, two ... eighteen, nineteen, twenty yuan. I put them in my piggy bank.

Zhang Peng: How many books do you have?

Boy: I have five.

Zhang Peng: How much is each book?

Boy: It's three yuan.

I pay fifteen yuan for five books. And I pay three yuan for a ball. I put two yuan back in my piggy bank.

词库：spend fifteen yuan　eighteen yuan　three yuan　twenty yuan　twelve yuan　two yuan

Look, choose and write.

How many books do you have?

I have ______ books.

How much is each book?

It's ______ yuan.

How many pencils do you have?

I have ______ pencils.

词库：three　five　eleven　twelve　thirteen　twenty

### Self-assessment

1. I can use the numbers 11 to 20 to count things.
2. I can use numbers to buy or sell things.
3. I can use the numbers 11 to 20 in different places.
4. I can read and spell words with "c", "g", "l" and "y".

## Reading time

（配图：生活中的数字主题图——NUMBERS IN LIFE 生活中的数字）

Numbers are useful. And numbers are interesting too.

How many eggs do we have?

Twelve.

A clock has twelve numbers. We use them to tell the time. And a year has twelve months!

In a mobile phone number, how many numbers are there?

Twelve?

No, eleven.

And a football team always has eleven players.

That's not true!

Oh, how so?

A player can get a red card!

That's true. Then the team only has ten players!
