# Unit 5 Old toys

How can we make things useful again?

（配图：单元主题图——印有 "DONATE 捐赠" 的旧物捐赠箱）

---

## Part A

### Look and think

- What do you see in the box?
- What toys do you have?

### Listen and chant

I have dolls.
I have balls.
Now I'm tall.
I give the balls,
And the dolls,
To my brother Paul.
I give them all.

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Giving old things new life

### Let's talk

Wow! So many toys!

That's right. They're my old toys. Do you have old things?

Yes, I do. I have some old books.

This boat is cool.

You can keep it.

Thanks!

### Think and say

（配图：分类框——School things／Toys）

Do you have any old school things?

Yes, I do. I have some pencils, ...

### Let's learn

Do you have old things at home?

Yes, I do. I have many old toys and books.

（配图：词汇图——School things／Toys 分类的旧物）ball　boat　car

### Listen and chant

I have a ball.
I have a doll.
I have a car.
I have a boat.
I have many toys.
They are all around.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

One cute cat uses two old balls.
Two cute cats use three old balls.
Three cute cats use four old balls.
Four cute cats use five old balls.
Five cute cats use six old balls.
So many cute cats, let's count them all.

Listen, circle and say.

（配图：u-e 发音练习图）

Listen, circle and write.

（配图：u-e 书写练习图）

u-e　　u-e　　u-e　　u-e

## Part B

### Let's talk

Mum, where is my animal book?

Is it on the shelf?

No, it isn't.

Aha! It's in the box. And this old cap too.

### Play a guessing game

（配图：猜位置游戏图——Toys）

Where is the doll?

Is it in the box?

No, it isn't.

Is it in the bag?

Yes, it is.

### Let's learn

Where is my map?

It's under the box.

（配图：方位词汇图）cap　map　in　on　under

### Listen and do

Up, up, up. Put your old map on the wall.
Down, down, down. Put your books under the ball.

Up, up, up. Put your old dolls on the shelf.
Down, down, down. Put your boat under the bed.

Up, up, up. Put your old things in a box.
Down, down, down. Sit down and talk.

### Read and write

Read and match.

Where do old things go?

I have some old cars and boats. They are still good. I put them on the shelves.

I have some old dolls and animal toys. They are still nice. I put them in a box.

I have many old books. They are still good. I put them in a box.

（配图：连线题——give away／take to school／sell 三种去向配对）

Look, choose and write.

Where is the ball?

It's ______ the map.

Is the ruler on the book?

No, it's ______ the book.

词库：in　on　under

Do you have old things at home?

Yes. I have a ______ and some ______.

词库：ball　boat　doll　cap　car　map

## Project: Reuse some old things

What old things does Mike have? Listen, circle the words and match.

（配图：听音圈词连线——boat　ball　books　cap　car　doll　map）

Are these toys or school things? Sort them.

ball　book　boat　car　doll　map　pencil box　pencil　pen　ruler

（配图：分类框——Toys／School things）

Do you have old things at home?

Yes, I have.

Reuse some things at home.

1. Find some old things at home.
2. Group the old things and put them in different boxes.
3. Reuse these things.

Share your work in class.

What's this?

It's a fish.

What are these?

They're boats.

### Self-assessment

1. I can describe old things I have.
2. I can ask and say where things are.
3. I can share ideas about reusing things.
4. I can read and spell words with "u-e".

## Reading time

（配图：Zoom 修机器人漫画）

Where is my boat? Is it in this box? Wow! My old robot!

Oh no! My robot has one arm, one eye and one foot. I need some help.

Hi, Cat! Do you have any old toys? I need an arm for my robot.

I have an old doll's arm.

Nice!

Thank you.

Hi, Tiger! Do you have any old toys? I need an eye for my robot.

I have an old car. You can use the light!

I also need a foot for my robot.

How about this?

That works!

Wow! Look at my robot now!
