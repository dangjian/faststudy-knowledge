# Unit 3 Learning better

What helps us learn?

（配图：单元主题图——平板上写着 "I have a pen."，周围摆满文具）

---

## Part A

### Look and think

- What do you see in the picture?
- What's in your school bag?

### Listen and chant

What helps us learn?
I hear with my ears.
I see with my eyes.
What helps us learn?
I use rulers and crayons.
I use pencils and pens.

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Can I use your pen?

### Let's talk

Excuse me. Can I use your eraser, please?

Sure. Here you are.

I can't find my ruler. Can I use your ruler, Zhang Peng?

No problem.

Thanks.

### Ask and answer

Can I use your eraser, please?

Here you are.

### Let's learn

I have a pen, a pencil, ...

（配图：文具词汇图）bag　book　pencil　paper　pen　ruler

### Listen and do

Read your book.

Write on your paper.

Write with your pencil.

Draw a line with your ruler.

Put the pen in your pencil box.

Put everything in your bag.

Draw a line.（画一条线。）

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

Let's make a kite,
Write number nine.
The kite is nice!
High five! High five!

Read, listen and tick.

1. big　bike
2. sit　like
3. lip　fine
4. fin　size

Listen, circle and write.

（配图：i-e 发音练习图）

i-e　　i-e　　i-e　　i-e

## Part B

### Let's talk

What's this?

It's a pencil.

What are these?

They're grapes.

Quack! Quack! What about this?

Aha! It's a duck!

### Play a guessing game

（配图：猜谜游戏图）

What are these?

They're ...

### Let's learn

What's this?

（配图：感官动词词汇图）see　smell　taste　hear　touch

### Listen and do

See some fruit,
Touch and feel.
Taste an apple,
Hear the sound.
Take an orange,
Smell the peel.

### Read and write

Read and match.

I smell with my nose.

I taste with my tongue.

I see and hear in class.

I can use paper, pencils, books and computers to learn.

（配图：连线题配图）

Look, choose and write.

What are these?

They're ______ and some ______.

词库：bag　book　paper　pen　pencil　ruler

Can I use your ______, please?

What helps you learn?

I can ______ with my ______.

词库：see　smell　touch　hear　／　ears　eyes　hands　nose

## Project: Make a sense book

What do the children need for their books? Listen, circle the pictures and match.

（配图：听音圈图连线——bag　book　eraser　paper　pen　pencil　ruler）

What do you see in the picture? Ask and answer. Then read the sentences aloud.

（配图：春日情景图）

- What are these?　They're rabbits.
- I hear birds.
- I taste an apple.
- I smell flowers.
- I touch the grass.
- I feel the sun.
- I see spring.

Make your sense book.

（配图：感官书示例）I see teachers and students. I hear songs. I love my school!

Share your book in class.

This is my sense book. I see ...

### Self-assessment

1. I can ask others politely to share school things.
2. I can describe how I use my senses.
3. I can describe how school things and the five senses help me learn.
4. I can read and spell words with "i-e".

## Reading time

（配图：动物与人类学习方式对比漫画）

We learn from Mum and Dad. Polar bears do the same.

We play and learn. Lions do the same.

We taste food with our tongues. Frogs get food with their tongues.

We speak and learn. Ants can't speak. But they can use smell to learn.

We draw maps with pencils and paper. Bees can't draw. But they can make a "map".

Eagles can see a rabbit from the sky. We can't. But we can use tools to see more!
