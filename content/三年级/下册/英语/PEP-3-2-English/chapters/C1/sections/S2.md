## Part B

How can we be polite?

### Let's talk

Let me help.

Thank you.

After you!

You're welcome.

Who's that girl?

That's our new neighbour, Amy.

### Choose and say

（配图：情景选择图）

1. After you.
2. You're welcome.
3. Let me help.
4. Thank you.

### Let's learn

Hi, Zhang Peng!

Hi!

（配图：人物词汇图）boy　girl　woman　man

Who's that man?

That's my new neighbour, Mr Lin.

### Listen and do

The man is my neighbour. The woman is my neighbour.
Nice to see you. How are you today?

The boy is my neighbour. The girl is my neighbour.
Nice to see you too. Let's go out and play!

### Read and write

Read, look and match.

Mike is my classmate. He is from Canada.

Zhang Peng is my classmate. He is my neighbour too.

Miss White is my English teacher. She is very nice.

（配图：连线题配图）Let me help.　　I'm from Canada.　　Hi! What's your name?　　Mum, I like my new school.

Look, choose and write.

Who's that boy?

That's Mike. He's from ______.

Who's that woman?

That's Miss White. She's an English ______.

词库：Canada　China　girl　man　student　teacher

Where are you from?

I'm from ______.

## Project: Make a poster for the food festival

The children meet some new people at the food festival. Listen and match.

（配图：听音连线图——the girl / the boy / the woman / the man 与 China / UK / Canada / USA 配对）

What happens at the food festival? Look and role-play.

Hi! I'm John.

Where are you from?

I'm from Henan, China.

My name is Linlin.

Sawat di kha! Hello! I'm from Thailand.

Do you like pineapple rice?

After you.

Yes.

Thank you.

Make your poster for the food festival.

（配图：美食节海报示例——四位小朋友自我介绍）
- Hi! I'm Malee. I'm from Thailand.
- Sawat di kha! I'm Mary. I'm from the USA.
- Nihao! I'm Linlin. I'm from China.
- Hello! I'm Amy. I'm from the UK.
- Hi! I'm Mike. I'm from Canada.
- Welcome to China!

Share your poster in class.

- What's this boy's/girl's name?　He's/She's ...
- Where is he/she from?　He's/She's from ...

### Self-assessment

1. I can greet new people and politely offer help.
2. I can say the name of different countries.
3. I can introduce my teachers and classmates.
4. I can read and spell words with "a-e".
