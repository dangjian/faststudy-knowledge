## Part A

### Look and think

（配图：男孩 Luke 的照片）

- What can you tell about this boy?
- What would you say to him?

### Listen and chant

Say "Hello!" and be nice.
A new friend is here.
Give it a try!

Wave your hand and be nice.
A new friend is here.
Don't be shy!

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Where are you from?

### Let's talk

Hi! I'm Zhang Peng.

My name's Amy Green.

What's your name?

I'm new here.

Me too!

Where are you from?

I'm from the UK.

What about you?

I'm from Shandong, China.
