# Unit 4 Healthy food

What is healthy food?

（配图：单元主题图——面包、面条、蔬菜、鸡蛋等健康食物）

---

## Part A

### Look and think

- What food do you see in the picture?
- What food is good for you?

### Listen and chant

Food, food,
I love healthy food.
I like rice and meat.
I love vegetables and fruit.
What is good?
Healthy food!

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Would you like some bread?

### Let's talk

Breakfast time!

OK, Mum. I'd like some bread and eggs, please.

Here you are.

Have some milk too.

Thanks.

### Let's play

I'd like some bread.

I'd like some bread and eggs.

I'd like some bread, eggs and cake.

### Let's learn

I'd like some milk and bread.

I'd like some juice and noodles.

（配图：菜单词汇图——noodles ¥16，egg ¥2.5，cake ¥10，milk ¥8.5，juice，bread）

### Listen and do

Eat your egg. Eat, eat, eat!

Drink your milk. Drink, drink, drink!

Cut your bread. Cut, cut, cut!

Eat your noodles. Eat, eat, eat!

Drink your juice. Drink, drink, drink!

Share the cake. Yummy!

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

Joe Jones eats at home,
Eat less junk food!
He likes to eat a very big cone.
Healthy food is no joke.
Less junk food, Joe. I hope.

Read, listen and circle.

1. John　Jones
2. hot　home
3. not　note
4. box　bone

Listen, circle and write.

（配图：o-e 发音练习图）

o-e　　o-e　　o-e　　o-e

## Part B

### Let's talk

Would you like some rice and meat?

Here you are.

Thanks, Mum.

Yes, please.

Have some vegetables too. They're good.

Thank you. I love vegetables.

### Role-play

Would you like some rice?

Yes, please.

Would you like some cake?

No, thank you.

### Let's learn

This is my healthy plate. I have vegetables, ...

（配图：食物词汇图）rice　soup　fruit　meat　vegetables

### Listen and chant

We are healthy. What do we need?
Water, soup, milk and juice.
We are healthy. What do we need?
Vegetables, rice, meat and fruit.

### Read and write

Read and circle the food in the pictures.

Rice and noodles are good. Meat, milk and eggs are good too.

Fruit and vegetables are colourful and healthy. Eat some every day!

Candy and cake are yummy, but don't eat too much!

（配图：三段短文配食物图，圈出文中提到的食物）

Look, choose and write.

Would you like some noodles?

No, thanks. I'd like some ______ and ______.

词库：bread　juice　meat　eggs　milk　noodles　rice　soup　vegetables

What about you?

I'd like some ______ and ______.
