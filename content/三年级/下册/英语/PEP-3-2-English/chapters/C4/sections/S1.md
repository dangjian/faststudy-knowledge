## Part A

### Look and think

- What food do you see in the picture?
- What food is good for you?

### Listen and chant

Food, food,
I love healthy food.
I like rice and meat.
I love vegetables and fruit.
What is good?
Healthy food!

### Listen and sing

（配图：歌曲页面插图）

Would you like some bread?

### Let's talk

Breakfast time!

OK, Mum. I'd like some bread and eggs, please.

Here you are.

Have some milk too.

Thanks.

### Let's play

I'd like some bread.

I'd like some bread and eggs.

I'd like some bread, eggs and cake.

### Let's learn

I'd like some milk and bread.

I'd like some juice and noodles.

（配图：菜单词汇图——noodles ¥16，egg ¥2.5，cake ¥10，milk ¥8.5，juice，bread）

### Listen and do

Eat your egg. Eat, eat, eat!

Drink your milk. Drink, drink, drink!

Cut your bread. Cut, cut, cut!

Eat your noodles. Eat, eat, eat!

Drink your juice. Drink, drink, drink!

Share the cake. Yummy!

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

Joe Jones eats at home,
Eat less junk food!
He likes to eat a very big cone.
Healthy food is no joke.
Less junk food, Joe. I hope.

Read, listen and circle.

1. John　Jones
2. hot　home
3. not　note
4. box　bone

Listen, circle and write.

（配图：o-e 发音练习图）
