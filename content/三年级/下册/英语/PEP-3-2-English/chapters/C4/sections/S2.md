## Part B

### Let's talk

Would you like some rice and meat?

Here you are.

Thanks, Mum.

Yes, please.

Have some vegetables too. They're good.

Thank you. I love vegetables.

### Role-play

Would you like some rice?

Yes, please.

Would you like some cake?

No, thank you.

### Let's learn

This is my healthy plate. I have vegetables, ...

（配图：食物词汇图）rice　soup　fruit　meat　vegetables

### Listen and chant

We are healthy. What do we need?
Water, soup, milk and juice.
We are healthy. What do we need?
Vegetables, rice, meat and fruit.

### Read and write

Read and circle the food in the pictures.

Rice and noodles are good. Meat, milk and eggs are good too.

Fruit and vegetables are colourful and healthy. Eat some every day!

Candy and cake are yummy, but don't eat too much!

（配图：三段短文配食物图，圈出文中提到的食物）

Look, choose and write.

Would you like some noodles?

No, thanks. I'd like some ______ and ______.

词库：bread　juice　meat　eggs　milk　noodles　rice　soup　vegetables

What about you?

I'd like some ______ and ______.
