# Revision A great weekend plan

## Read aloud and act it out.

（配图：Sarah 一家和 Mike 一家分别讨论周末计划）

Mum: Let's take a farm trip this weekend. What's the weather like this weekend?

Dad: It's cloudy but warm.

Sarah: Can I wear a skirt and a T-shirt?

Mum: A skirt is not a good idea. Trousers or shorts are good.

Dad: It's sunny today. Let's go to the park on the hill.

Mike: Great! We can have a picnic.

Mum: Let's get ready. Wear your hiking shoes and take your water bottles.

Matt: Can I wear my hat and sunglasses?

Mum: Sure. That's a good idea.

Sam: Well, I'd like to wear my shorts.

Sarah's family are on the farm ...

"It's time to milk the cows," says Dad.

"Oh no! It's raining," says Sarah.

"It's OK. We'll milk the cows later. You can pick tomatoes in the greenhouse," says Mum.

The rain stops. It's time for dinner. "Great! Let's make a salad," says Sam. "Yummy!" Everyone is happy.

Mike's family are in the car museum ...

"Wow! There are so many old cars here!" says Mike.

"Can we go to the second floor? I like racing cars," says Matt.

Dad says, "OK. Matt and I can go together. Mike, you can go with your mum. What time is it now? It's 2:15. Let's meet here at 5:30. Then we can have dinner. Don't be late!" says Mum.
