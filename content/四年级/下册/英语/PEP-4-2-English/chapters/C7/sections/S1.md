## Read aloud and act it out.

（配图：Sarah 一家和 Mike 一家分别讨论周末计划）

Mum: Let's take a farm trip this weekend. What's the weather like this weekend?

Dad: It's cloudy but warm.

Sarah: Can I wear a skirt and a T-shirt?

Mum: A skirt is not a good idea. Trousers or shorts are good.

Dad: It's sunny today. Let's go to the park on the hill.

Mike: Great! We can have a picnic.

Mum: Let's get ready. Wear your hiking shoes and take your water bottles.

Matt: Can I wear my hat and sunglasses?

Mum: Sure. That's a good idea.

Sam: Well, I'd like to wear my shorts.
