# Appendix 1 Songs（歌曲）

## Unit 1

**Class rules**

Rules, rules, rules.
Rules, rules, rules.
Don't make a mess.
Don't forget to tidy your room!
What class rules does your school have?
Listen with care. Put up your hand!
Rules, rules, rules.
They're good, good, good.
Rules, rules, rules.
Rules, rules, rules.
What class rules does your school have?
Time to speak,
And time to listen.
Rules, rules, rules.
They're good, good, good.
Don't be late! Don't eat in class!

## Unit 2

**Rules at home**

There are lots of rules at home.
There are lots of things to do.
Tidy your room.
Clean the dishes.
Listen to your parents too.

There are lots of rules at home,
And things you cannot do.
Don't say bad words.

## Unit 3

**What time is it now?**

What time is it now?
It's time for breakfast.
Tra-la-la-la-la, la-la-la-la.
What time is it now?
It's time for Chinese.
Tra-la-la-la-la, la-la-la-la.
Time to speak,
And time to listen.
What time is it now?
It's time for English.
Tra-la-la-la-la, la-la-la-la.

## Unit 4

**How much is it?**

How much are the red shoes in the window?
They're nice. Can I buy a new pair?
How much are the red shoes in the window?
I hope that the price is quite fair.

How much is the blue hat in the window?
Oh, please, please tell me the price!
How much is the blue hat in the window?
Can I try it on? It looks really nice.

## Unit 5

**On the farm**

How many cows do you have on the farm?
How many cows do you have?
Ten in the field,
And eight in the shed.
That makes eighteen cows on the farm.

How many trees do you have on the farm?
How many trees do you have?
Five apple trees,
And ten pear trees.
That makes fifteen trees on the farm.

## Unit 6

**Farmers**

This is a farmer. He works hard.
He milks cows, and he plants his fruit.
He waters plants and picks them too.
All to make some good food for you.

This is a farmer. She works hard.
She feeds the pigs, and she plants her fruit.
She waters plants and picks them too.
All to make some good food for you.
