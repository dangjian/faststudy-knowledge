## Unit 5

**On the farm**

How many cows do you have on the farm?
How many cows do you have?
Ten in the field,
And eight in the shed.
That makes eighteen cows on the farm.

How many trees do you have on the farm?
How many trees do you have?
Five apple trees,
And ten pear trees.
That makes fifteen trees on the farm.

## Unit 6

**Farmers**

This is a farmer. He works hard.
He milks cows, and he plants his fruit.
He waters plants and picks them too.
All to make some good food for you.

This is a farmer. She works hard.
She feeds the pigs, and she plants her fruit.
She waters plants and picks them too.
All to make some good food for you.
