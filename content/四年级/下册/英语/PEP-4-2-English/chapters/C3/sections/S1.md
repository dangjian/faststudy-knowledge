## Part A What is your schedule?

**Look and think**

- What are the children doing at this time?
- What do you do at this time?

（配图：孩子们不同时段的活动场景）

**Listen and chant**

Move your hands round and round.
Tell the time on the clock.
Seven o'clock. Time to get up!
Eight o'clock. Time for school!
Twelve o'clock. Time for lunch!
Tick-tock. Time moves fast.
Move your hands round and round.
Tell the time on the clock.
Five o'clock. Time to go home!
Six o'clock. Time for dinner!
Nine o'clock. Time for bed!
Tick-tock. It's time to sleep.

**Listen and sing**

What time is it now?

### Let's talk

（配图：放学后的操场，Binbin 与同学对话，老师提醒回家）

Hi, Binbin. School is over. Let's go to the playground.

OK.

What time is it?

It's 5 o'clock.

Oh! Let's go.

Time to go home, kids.

What time is it now?

It's 6 o'clock. It's time for dinner.

### Choose and say

（配图：世界地图与时钟，北京、伦敦、纽约、悉尼）

- Beijing 7:10 p.m.
- London 12:10 p.m.
- New York 7:10 a.m.
- Sydney 9:10 p.m.

Where are you?

I'm in New York.

What time is it there?

It's 7:10 a.m. It's time for breakfast.

In Beijing, it's 7:10 p.m. ...

### Let's learn

（配图：课程与三餐场景配图）

- breakfast
- lunch
- dinner
- maths class
- English class
- art class

What time is it?

It's 7:20. It's time for breakfast.

**Listen and do**

It's time for breakfast. Let's drink some milk.

It's time for lunch. Let's have some chicken.

It's time for maths class. Let's count and write.（25+19）

It's time for dinner. Let's eat some rice.

It's time for art class. Let's draw a picture.

It's time for English class. Let's read and write.

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**or**

I'm Ford the hero. I get up early every morning.

I ride my horse to help others.

I work all over the world.

Ford! It's time to get up!

Read, listen and circle.

1. fork　horse
2. word　worth
3. work　world
4. sport　short

Look, listen and write.
