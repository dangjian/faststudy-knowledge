## Part B How do family rules help us?

### Let's talk

（配图：John 和 Jack 在家，爸爸在睡觉，妈妈在厨房）

John, where is Dad?

Shh. Don't be so loud! Dad is sleeping in his bedroom. He's tired.

Be careful, Jack. Don't touch hot things.

I'm sorry.

Where is Mum?

She's in the kitchen. Let's go and help her.

OK.

### Look and say

Don't touch hot things.

Don't ...

- do his homework

- together

Where is the boy?

He's in the living room. He's sweeping the floor.

### Read and write

Read, tick and underline.

（配图：短文配图）

Family rules
By Alan

There are rules at school and at home. I don't run in the classroom or in the living room. This rule keeps me safe. I keep my desk clean and tidy my bedroom. This rule keeps me healthy. I think of my friends and family. I use kind words.

We work hard and follow the rules. I feel good and others feel good too.

Tick the rules in the text.

- Be careful in the kitchen.
- Tidy your bedroom.
- Do your homework first.
- Use kind words.
- Keep your desk clean.
- Wash your hands.

Underline the reasons for the rules.

Look and write.

- Dad is reading in the study.
- My brother is playing in the living room.
- Don't be so loud!
- Do your homework first.

Write one rule you know.

### Project: Make family rules

What rules does the family have? Listen, tick and match.

（配图：Family rules 家庭规则图）

- Don't play with balls in the living room.
- Don't touch hot things.
- Wash your hands.
- Tidy your bedroom.
- Do your homework first.
- Use kind words.

What family rules do you have? Think and talk in pairs.

Binbin: What family rules do you have?

Mike: Don't jump on the bed. Don't eat in bed. ...

Binbin: Do you like them all?

Mike: "Don't eat in bed." is a good rule. But my brother doesn't like it.

Binbin: You can talk about it with your family.

Make your family rules.

（配图：My family rules 规则卡模板）

We agree to follow these family rules:

- Use kind words.
- Listen to each other.

（our names）:

Share your family rules in class.

We agree to follow these family rules. Use kind words and ...

**Self-check**

1. I can tell others some rules at home.
2. I can name rooms at home and say what I can and can't do there.
3. I can list some rules and say why they help us.
4. I can read and spell words with "er".

### Reading time

（配图：Jack 在家中各处玩耍，妈妈不断提醒的四格漫画）

In the study

No, Jack! Please don't make a mess!

Why not? I'm having fun!

In the living room

No, Jack! Please don't play football in the living room!

Why not? I'm having fun!

In the kitchen

No, Jack! Please don't be so loud!

Why not? I'm having fun!

In the bedroom

No, Jack! Please don't jump on the bed!

Why not? I'm having fun!

Why can't I have fun at home, Mum?

You can, but it has to be safe. You should also think of others.

Let's go to the park tomorrow. You can jump and have fun there!

Yeah!
