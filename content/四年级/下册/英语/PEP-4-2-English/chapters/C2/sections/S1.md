## Part A What are your family rules?

**Look and think**

- What do you see in these pictures?
- What are your family rules?

（配图：家庭生活场景）

**Listen and chant**

Rules at home. They keep us safe.
Don't run! Don't bounce the balls!
Listen to what Mum and Dad say.
Rules at home. They make us happy.
Don't make a mess! Think of others!
Use nice words and speak kindly.

**Listen and sing**

Rules at home

### Let's talk

（配图：Matt 在客厅和妈妈对话）

Mum, can I watch TV?

No. You have to do your homework first.

OK.

Matt, be careful! The floor is wet.

Sorry.

Don't run in the living room. It's not safe.

### Think and say

（配图：Family rules 与 Class rules 对比图）

Family rules

- Tidy your room.
- Don't run in the ...

Class rules

- Don't be late.
- Keep the classroom clean.

Tidy your room. Don't ...

### Let's learn

Family rules

（配图：Family rules 班规式配图）

- Do my homework first.
- Use kind words.
- Tidy my room.
- Wash my hands.

There are some rules in my family: Do my homework first. ... They are all helpful.

### Look, choose and say

（配图：孩子在家不同行为的场景图）

1. Use kind words.
2. Tidy your room.
3. Wash your hands.
4. Do your homework first.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**er**

In the summer, after dinner,
Father is reading a newspaper.
My poor thirsty brother,
Is running with some water.

Read, underline and listen.

1. brother　number　ruler　sister
2. computer　dinner　eraser　winter
3. after　clever　river　under　water

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

Zoom is drinking some ...

No water near the ...
