## Part B What can we learn on a farm?

### Let's talk

（配图：参观农场菜地与蜂箱）

What are these?

They're tomatoes.

Yes. A lot of tomatoes are big and red, but these are small and yellow.

But they're yellow!

What are those?

They're bee boxes. Bees are good for plants.

### Play a guessing game

What are those?

They're ...

（配图：动物叫声提示）

- cow: moo
- sheep: baa
- pig: oink
- horse: neigh

### Let's learn

A mouse!

Look at the tomatoes. They're big and red.

（配图：蔬菜词汇配图）

- tomatoes
- carrots
- potatoes

**Listen and chant**

I like long vegetables:
Cucumbers, carrots and green beans.
I like round vegetables:
Potatoes, tomatoes and green peas.
I like flowers and honey,
And so do the bees.
But mice like rice.
They eat anything they please!

### Read and write

Read and circle.

（配图：王先生的农场场景）

This is Mr Wang's farm. Let's have a look!

It's a busy day on the farm. Mr Wang is putting the tomatoes in boxes. These are carrots. Those are green beans and potatoes. How fresh! What are these? They are cans for milk. There are boxes of eggs too. It is a big year for Mr Wang.

Circle what you see on Mr Wang's farm.

| Vegetable | carrots　cucumbers　potatoes　tomatoes |
|------|------|
| Animal | horses　chickens　cows　sheep |

Look, draw and write.

These are sheep.

Draw some farm animals or vegetables and write one sentence about them.

### Project: Make a "farm-to-table" flow chart

The children are visiting a farm. Listen and choose.

Which farm are they talking about?

Where does our food come from? Look, match and say.

（配图：食物图片）

- chicken
- beef

- rice
- mantou
- sweet potato
- baozi

My family often eat rice, chicken and tomatoes for lunch. These foods are all from farms.

Make your flow chart.

Where does our food come from?

（配图：farm-to-table 流程图模板）

- Farms give us food on our table.
- There are ______ and tomato plants on farms.
- Farms give us milk, ______ and ______.
- The food in the supermarket is from ______.

Share your flow chart in class.

We need farms. A lot of our food is from farms. Cows and chickens live there. They give us milk, eggs and meat.

**Self-check**

1. I can talk about different plants and animals on a farm.
2. I can say the names of animals and vegetables on a farm.
3. I can explain why we need farms.
4. I can read and spell words with "air".

### Reading time

（配图：食物与能量科普短文配图）

You need energy to have fun and to study. But where is this energy from?

Milk, eggs, meat, fruit and vegetables can give you energy.

So where is your food from? A lot of it comes from farms.

Some farms are small, and farmers there only grow one thing.

Some farms are large. Farmers there grow plants like corn, tomatoes, rice and carrots. They also raise animals like cows, chickens and pigs.

Remember, plants and animals take months or years to grow. So don't waste food!
