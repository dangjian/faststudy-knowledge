## Part A What farm animals do you know?

**Look and think**

- What do you see in the picture?
- Which foods grow on farms?

（配图：农场食物场景）

**Listen and chant**

Farms give us potatoes.
Farms give us tomatoes.
Farms give us fruit and vegetables.
They are really great!
Farms have cows and chickens.
Farms give us meat and eggs.
Farms are really helpful.
We all need farms to eat!

**Listen and sing**

On the farm

### Let's talk

（配图：参观农场，与农场主对话）

What animals do you have on the farm?

I have a lot of animals. Cows, horses, sheep, pigs and chickens.

How many cows do you have?

I have 20.

Cows are great. They give us milk.

Yes. Farm animals give us many things.

### Ask and answer

What animals do you have on the farm?

I have cows.

How many cows do you have?

I have 15 cows.

（配图：农场动物图（cows））

### Let's learn

（配图：农场动物词汇配图）

- horses
- pigs
- chickens
- cows
- sheep

I have a lot of animals.

Yes. There are sheep, cows ...

### Read and guess

I like to run fast. I am fun to ride. What am I?

I can make milk. I am black and white. What am I?

I can lay eggs. I am a bird, but I don't fly well. What am I?

I like the mud. I have a big nose and small eyes. What am I?

My coat can keep you warm. I am woolly and white. What am I?

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**air**

A boy with fair hair,
Sits down on his chair.
The chair is small.
The poor boy falls.
His hat is in the air!

Read, listen and number.

chair　fair　air　hair　pair
