# Unit 4 Going shopping

（配图：一家四口在服装店橱窗前选购的单元主题图）How do people make choices?

---

## Part A What do we need to buy?

**Look and think**

- What are the people doing?
- How do you choose your clothes?

（配图：人们购物场景）

**Listen and chant**

We like new clothes,
To wear in different places.
We like beautiful colours,
To wear on different days.
It's fun to choose new clothes.
These new clothes are nice.
How much are they?
Let's look at the price!

**Listen and sing**

How much is it?

### Let's talk

（配图：Mike 和妈妈在服装店）

Mum, my trousers are too short. Can I buy a new pair?

Sure. Let's go to the clothes shop.

Look, Mum! Those shorts are nice. Can I buy a pair?

You already have too many shorts. Let's buy trousers.

OK, Mum.

### Read and role-play

（配图：购物清单 Shopping list）

- pen
- toy car
- pencil box
- book
- dog
- trousers
- cap
- shorts
- coat

Mum, can I buy a new coat? This one is too small.

Sure. You need a new coat. Let's go shopping.

Can I buy a toy car too?

You already have too many toy cars. Let's just buy the coat.

### Let's learn

Clothes

（配图：服装词汇配图）

- trousers
- jacket
- skirt
- shorts

My skirt is too small. That one is nice. I need a new one.

### Choose and say

What clothes do they need?

（配图：Picnic 与 School trip 场景）

Picnic：I need a pair of shorts and a ...

School trip：I need my school uniform.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**ir / ur**

A girl buys a pretty skirt.
She sees a bird — it's hurt.
First she thinks, then helps the bird.
Now the girl needs a new skirt!

Read, listen and circle.

1. bird　hurt
2. shirt　girl
3. fur　nurse
4. skirt　turn

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

The ______ is dirty.

______ gets a new skirt.

## Part B How do we decide what to buy?

### Let's talk

（配图：服装店里 Sarah 与妈妈、店员对话）

Can I help you?

Yes. I like this pink dress.

How much is it?

It's 180 yuan.

I'm sorry, dear. That's expensive.

Look! The blue skirt is nice too. It's 79 yuan.

Yes. I like it.

OK. Let's take it.

### Read and role-play

（配图：打折价签与购物小票）

How much is the jacket?

It's 50 yuan now.

| Item | Qty. | Disc. | Price |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| Jacket |  | 50% | ￥50 |
| Socks |  | 20% | ￥20 |
| Skirt |  | 0% | ￥79 |

Total: ￥149

OK. Let's take it.

### Let's learn

（配图：商品与价格标签配图）

- expensive（￥75）
- cheap（￥45）
- shoes
- hat
- beautiful
- sunglasses（￥80）

Do you like the sunglasses?

Yes! They're beautiful. They're 99 yuan.

How much are they?

### Look and role-play

（配图：商品价签：￥78、￥80、￥88、￥62、￥20 等）

Can I help you?

Yes. I like the blue shorts.

How much are they?

They're 20 yuan.

That's cheap!

### Read and write

Read and answer.

（配图：SALES 促销海报与妈妈的购物便条）

Dear Mike,

Here's your shopping list.

1. Sunglasses — Buy a nice and cheap pair. Sunglasses: Sixty yuan now!
2. Shorts — Buy two pairs. Try them on first. Shorts: Fifteen yuan each!
3. A hat — Find your size. You can choose any colour. Hats: Large (L) size only, ten yuan!

Don't buy socks! You have a lot of socks!

Mum

1. What can Mike buy for 90 yuan?
2. Are the hats good for everyone?

Look and write.

How much are these shorts and sunglasses?

The shorts are ten yuan.

（配图：商品价签 ￥20）

Think of one thing you have and write about it.

### Project: Choose clothes for a singing contest

The children are choosing clothes for a contest. Listen, tick and match.

（配图：服装图片）

- dress
- shirt
- hat
- shorts
- shoes
- skirt
- sunglasses
- trousers

Look at each set. Then make a choice.

（配图：三套服装套装及价格：Set 1 ￥30/￥20/￥35，Set 2 ￥60，Set 3 ￥25/￥25/￥25）

I like the shirt with the black tie. Can we buy Set 2?

How much is it?

It's 60 yuan.

We only have 50 yuan each.

Write the choices for the singing contest.

**Self-check**

1. I can ask about prices and decide what to buy.
2. I can talk about clothes and other items.
3. I can share ideas on how to shop wisely.
4. I can read and spell words with "ir" and "ur".

### Reading time

（配图：Ben 看中一辆红色自行车，与爸爸对话的漫画）

Dad, I love the red bike. Can I save up my money and buy it?

Yes. That's a great idea, Ben.

Two months later

Mum, Dad! I can buy the red bike soon.

Oh no! The red bike is not here.

I'm sorry.

Wow! It's the red bike!

You work hard and often help Mum and me. This is our special gift for you.

Thank you so much, Dad! I still have all this money. Please take it!

No. It's your money. Save it for a rainy day.

I will, Dad. Thanks.
