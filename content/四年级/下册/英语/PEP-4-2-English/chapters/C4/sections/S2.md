## Part B How do we decide what to buy?

### Let's talk

（配图：服装店里 Sarah 与妈妈、店员对话）

Can I help you?

Yes. I like this pink dress.

How much is it?

It's 180 yuan.

I'm sorry, dear. That's expensive.

Look! The blue skirt is nice too. It's 79 yuan.

Yes. I like it.

OK. Let's take it.

### Read and role-play

（配图：打折价签与购物小票）

How much is the jacket?

It's 50 yuan now.

| Item | Qty. | Disc. | Price |

| Jacket |  | 50% | ￥50 |
| Socks |  | 20% | ￥20 |

OK. Let's take it.

### Look and role-play

（配图：商品价签：￥78、￥80、￥88、￥62、￥20 等）

Can I help you?

Yes. I like the blue shorts.

How much are they?

They're 20 yuan.

That's cheap!

### Read and write

Read and answer.

（配图：SALES 促销海报与妈妈的购物便条）

Dear Mike,

Here's your shopping list.

1. Sunglasses — Buy a nice and cheap pair. Sunglasses: Sixty yuan now!
2. Shorts — Buy two pairs. Try them on first. Shorts: Fifteen yuan each!
3. A hat — Find your size. You can choose any colour. Hats: Large (L) size only, ten yuan!

Don't buy socks! You have a lot of socks!

Mum

1. What can Mike buy for 90 yuan?
2. Are the hats good for everyone?

Look and write.

How much are these shorts and sunglasses?

The shorts are ten yuan.

（配图：商品价签 ￥20）

Think of one thing you have and write about it.

### Project: Choose clothes for a singing contest

The children are choosing clothes for a contest. Listen, tick and match.

（配图：服装图片）

- dress
- shirt
- hat
- shorts
- shoes
- skirt
- sunglasses
- trousers

Look at each set. Then make a choice.

（配图：三套服装套装及价格：Set 1 ￥30/￥20/￥35，Set 2 ￥60，Set 3 ￥25/￥25/￥25）

I like the shirt with the black tie. Can we buy Set 2?

How much is it?

It's 60 yuan.

We only have 50 yuan each.

Write the choices for the singing contest.

### Reading time

（配图：Ben 看中一辆红色自行车，与爸爸对话的漫画）

Dad, I love the red bike. Can I save up my money and buy it?

Yes. That's a great idea, Ben.

Two months later

Mum, Dad! I can buy the red bike soon.

Oh no! The red bike is not here.

I'm sorry.

Wow! It's the red bike!

You work hard and often help Mum and me. This is our special gift for you.

Thank you so much, Dad! I still have all this money. Please take it!

No. It's your money. Save it for a rainy day.

I will, Dad. Thanks.
