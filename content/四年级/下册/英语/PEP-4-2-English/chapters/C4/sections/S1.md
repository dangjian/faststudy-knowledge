## Part A What do we need to buy?

**Look and think**

- What are the people doing?
- How do you choose your clothes?

（配图：人们购物场景）

**Listen and chant**

We like new clothes,
To wear in different places.
We like beautiful colours,
To wear on different days.
It's fun to choose new clothes.
These new clothes are nice.
How much are they?
Let's look at the price!

**Listen and sing**

How much is it?

### Let's talk

（配图：Mike 和妈妈在服装店）

Mum, my trousers are too short. Can I buy a new pair?

Sure. Let's go to the clothes shop.

Look, Mum! Those shorts are nice. Can I buy a pair?

You already have too many shorts. Let's buy trousers.

OK, Mum.

### Read and role-play

（配图：购物清单 Shopping list）

- pen
- toy car
- pencil box
- book
- dog
- trousers
- cap
- shorts
- coat

Mum, can I buy a new coat? This one is too small.

Sure. You need a new coat. Let's go shopping.

Can I buy a toy car too?

You already have too many toy cars. Let's just buy the coat.

### Let's learn

Clothes

（配图：服装词汇配图）

- trousers
- jacket
- skirt
- shorts

My skirt is too small. That one is nice. I need a new one.

### Choose and say

What clothes do they need?

（配图：Picnic 与 School trip 场景）

Picnic：I need a pair of shorts and a ...

School trip：I need my school uniform.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**ir / ur**

A girl buys a pretty skirt.
She sees a bird — it's hurt.
First she thinks, then helps the bird.
Now the girl needs a new skirt!

Read, listen and circle.

1. bird　hurt
2. shirt　girl
3. fur　nurse
4. skirt　turn

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

The ______ is dirty.

______ gets a new skirt.
