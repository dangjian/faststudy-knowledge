## Part A What are your class rules?

（配图：教室里孩子们纷纷举手发言的单元主题图）Why are class rules important?

**Look and think**

- What are the students doing?
- What class rules do you know?

（配图：学生课堂活动场景）

**Listen and chant**

We follow rules to help us learn.
Put up your hand and take your turn.
We listen, ask and answer.
This all makes learning fun.
We follow rules to help us learn.
Don't be late or eat in class.
We don't want a messy room.
Our classroom is for everyone.

**Listen and sing**

Class rules

### Let's talk

（配图：Jack 背着书包准备上学，手里拿着玩具和苹果，与同学对话）

Jack, you can't take toys to school.

Oh, sorry. Can I take this apple?

Yes, but don't eat it in class.

OK.

Hurry up! Don't be late for class!

I'm ready. Let's go!

### Choose, talk and group

What's this? "Please" or "Don't"?

（配图：词卡）

- be late for class
- Please be nice.
- eat in class
- Thanks. It's OK.
- take toys to school
- be nice
- keep the classroom clean
- run in the classroom

### Let's learn

These are our class rules.

（配图：Class rules 班规配图）

- Keep the classroom clean.
- Don't eat in class.
- Don't be late.
- Put up your hand to speak.
- Turn off the lights after school.

### Look and say

What can we say to these students?

（配图：学生在教室中的不同行为场景）

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**ar**

I draw a farmer on a card. The farmer is working hard. His farm is like a big park.

I clean my desk after class. It's not hard.

Read, listen and number.

far　card　farm　arm　car　hard
