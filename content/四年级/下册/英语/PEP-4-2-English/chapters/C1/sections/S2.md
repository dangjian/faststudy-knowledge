## Part B Why do we follow class rules?

### Let's talk

（配图：教室里两名值日生在打扫，Miss White 在询问）

Who's on duty today?

We are, Miss White.

I can clean the blackboard.

I can sweep the floor.

I can put back the desks and chairs.

Wow! Our classroom is clean and tidy!

### Look and say

What can you do after the English party?

I can ...

### Let's learn

Look! The music room is nice and clean.

（配图：音乐室场景，标注物品名称）

- window
- teacher's desk
- fan
- door
- chairs

I like this room. The floor is green. The chairs are ...

**Listen and do**

We turn off the fans.

We clean the desks.

We sweep the floor.

We close the windows.

We put back the chairs and close the doors.

Our job is done!

Now the classroom is clean!

### Read and write

Read and tick.

（配图：短文配图）

Helping hands in class

We have some class rules. We put up our hands to speak. We turn off the lights after school. When we don't understand, we say, "Excuse me?" We also have some jobs. Some of us make wall newspapers. Some of us keep our classroom clean and tidy. Some hand out the workbooks. We all help make our classroom great!

Tick the class rules in the text.

- Say "Excuse me?"
- Turn off the lights after school.
- Close the windows.
- Put up your hand to speak.
- Don't eat in class.
- Keep the classroom clean.

Look and write.

（配图：My class rules 书写框）

- Put up your hand to speak.
- Turn off the lights after school.
- Don't eat in class.
- Don't be late.

Read the class rules. Then list your own rules.

Class rules

Make some signs for your class rules.

Share your signs in class.

Look at this sign. What rule is it?

Keep the classroom clean.

Yes, you're right! Let's put it on the wall.

**Self-check**

1. I can talk about class rules.
2. I can say classroom items and class duties.
3. I can tell people what class rules are and why we follow them.
4. I can read and spell words with "ar".
