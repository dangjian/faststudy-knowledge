# Appendix 5 Pronunciation（语音）

## 单词重音

worker　basketball　newspaper

police　potato　expensive

Listen and choose.

away　beautiful　tomato

## 句子重读

My 'father is a 'doctor.

I 'pay 'fifteen 'yuan for 'five 'books.

It's 'not 'safe.

'What 'colour is it?

Is it cold and windy in Beijing?

He often helps me with English.

Listen and read aloud. Notice the stress.

## 连读

Good morning! Come in.

Is it on the shelf?

Can I take this apple?

My neighbour is a police officer.

Listen and read aloud. Notice the linking.

## 意群

I'm making the bed | for the old people.

I have to keep my desk clean | and tidy my bedroom.

A lot of tomatoes are big and red, | but these are small and yellow.

It's fun to go to the park | with my new friends.

She makes a salad | and eats it with her family.

Candy and cake are yummy, | but don't eat too much!

Listen and read aloud. Notice the pause.
