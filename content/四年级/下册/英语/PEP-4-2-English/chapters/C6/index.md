# Unit 6 From farm to table

（配图：农民手捧稻穗的单元主题图）How do we get food?

---

## Part A What can we do on a farm?

**Look and think**

- What food do we get from this plant?
- What do farmers often do on farms?

（配图：稻田与农场劳作场景）

**Listen and chant**

What do farmers do?
They work hard for us all.
When do they work?
From winter to fall.
What do they plant?
Fruit, vegetables and more!
What do they feed?
Cows, pigs and more.
Farmers work so hard.
Don't waste food anymore!

**Listen and sing**

Farmers

### Let's talk

（配图：农场里 Binbin 和 John 在给蔬菜浇水，大家帮忙喂鸡喂猪）

Look! Binbin and John are watering the vegetables.

Oh yes! We can help too. Let's feed the chickens.

Good idea! Let's feed the pigs.

OK. Can you please pass me the vegetables?

Sure. Here you are.

Thanks.

### Ask and answer

Let's feed the chickens.

Here are some vegetables.

Thanks.

### Let's learn

（配图：农场劳作场景配图）

- Farmers plant potatoes.
- They water carrots.
- They pick tomatoes.
- They feed chickens.
- They milk cows.

Farmers do a lot of work on the farm.

**Listen and do**

Plant the tomatoes.

Water the tomatoes.

Pick the tomatoes.

Feed the cow.

Give the cow water.

Milk the cow.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**ear**

My dear bear, do you hear?
A chicken clucks very near.
I hear! It's so clear!
Oh dear! So many eggs here and there!
And near the eggs, there is a pear!

Read, listen and circle.

1. bear　ear
2. dear　wear
3. pear　fear
4. year　hear

Look, listen and write.

He ______ is eating a ______.

Happy Chinese New ______, Grandma!

## Part B How should we eat?

### Let's talk

（配图：王先生家聚餐，Mike 和 Amy 用餐）

Mike, Amy, would you like a knife and fork?

No, thank you, Mr Wang. I can use chopsticks.

Can you please pass me some chopsticks too?

OK.

The food is delicious! Don't waste food, please.

Thank you! Sorry.

Let's clear the table.

OK.

### Ask and answer

Would you like some vegetables?

Yes, please.

Can you please pass me the fork?

Sure.

### Let's learn

Let's set the table.

Let's clear the table. Can you please pass me the bowl?

OK.

Sure.

（配图：餐具词汇配图）

- knife
- chopsticks
- bowl
- fork
- spoon

### Think and say

Good table manners

（配图：餐桌礼仪图）

- Set the table before dinner.
- Clear the table after dinner.
- Don't play with your knife/fork.
- Don't point chopsticks at others.
- Don't waste food.

### Read and write

Read and underline.

（配图：陈杰在家中小花园种番茄的配图）

Where do you get your tomatoes? In a supermarket? At a farm? Chen Jie grows them by herself. She has a small "garden" at home. Spring is a good time to plant tomatoes. Chen Jie waters the tomato plants every week. In summer, Chen Jie picks the tomatoes. She makes a salad and eats it with her family. It's so delicious!

Find these actions in the text and underline them.

Look and write.

Let's set the table.

（配图：餐桌场景）

Write one more sentence about table manners.

### Project: Design a Family Farm Day ad

What are the people doing on the farm? Listen and tick.

What can you do on a farm? Choose and talk with a partner.

- Let's water the tomatoes/carrots.
- Let's pick the tomatoes/carrots.
- Let's feed the chickens/pigs.
- Can you please pass me the bowls/chopsticks/forks/knives/spoons?

Make a Family Farm Day ad.

（配图：Family Farm Day 广告模板）

Family Farm Day

Bring your family and have fun!

Every Saturday. A great time for the whole family

- Plant carrots/potatoes ...
- Water vegetables
- Pick fruit
- Feed farm animals
- Make bowls/spoons

Share your ad in class.

We can plant carrots, pick fruit, feed farm animals ...

We can also make bowls and ...

1. I can talk about farmers' activities.
2. I can name tableware and say how we should eat.
3. I can talk about how people grow tomatoes at home.
4. I can read and spell words with "ear".

### Reading time

（配图：各种新式农场科普配图）

There are many farms around us. Let's look at some of them. They can show us new ways to grow food.

Look! On this farm there is a robot farmer. It is planting potatoes.

Can you please pass me some potatoes?

Those robots are picking tomatoes very quickly.

Let's pick some tomatoes.

This robot does the work of bees. It helps plants grow fruit.

I can do a lot of work on the farm!

This farm is very big and high. It grows many vegetables without soil.

We have many farms, but there are so many people in the world. Don't waste your food. Help save food for others.
