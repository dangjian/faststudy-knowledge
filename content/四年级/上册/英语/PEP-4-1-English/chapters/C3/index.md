# Unit 3 Places we live in

（配图：单元主题图，一家人在小区步道上散步，孩子们玩滑板车）What makes a community?

---

## Part A What can we see in a community?

**Look and think**

- What is this place?
- What do people do here?

（配图：社区场景）

**Listen and chant**

What makes a community?
The animals or the families?
The park or the hospital?
The playground or the school?
We all make our community!
It's a place for us all!

**Listen and sing**

We love our community!

### Let's talk

（配图：小区里，祖孙俩与邻居问好）

Good afternoon!

Your neighbours are very friendly.

Yes, they are. There is a playground. We often play there.

Good afternoon!

That's nice. There is also a nice park over there. Let's go and see!

### Draw and say

There is a park in your community. I often play there with my friends.

（pet hospital　park）

### Let's learn

- hospital
- playground
- park
- shop
- toilets
- bus stop

（配图：社区场所词汇配图）

We're at the park. Look! There is a shop.

Great! Let's go and see.

### Choose, stick and say

There is a hospital. There is also a playground.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**ck**

Some ducks are in the park.
The poor ducklings feel cold there.
Some nice children go back home,
For black socks they can wear.

Read, listen and tick.

black　sock　back　duck　tick

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

## Part B What do people do in a community?

### Let's talk

（配图：图书馆里）

This is a library.

Wow! There are so many books.

There is a taijiquan club. There are many people.

There is a gym too.

Great! Let's do some sports.

### Look and say

There is a playground.

There are books, pencils and clothes.

### Let's learn

The community and us

- go shopping
- take a walk
- do sports
- see a doctor

There is a playground in my community. I often do sports there.

**Listen and do**

There is a beautiful park. I often take a walk there.

There is a playground. I do sports there.

There is a shop. I go shopping there.

There is a hospital. I see a doctor there.

### Read and write

Read and tick.

**My favourite place is the museum.** There are many old things and photos. I can read stories about them. I can "hear" them too! There is also a nice shop. People buy gifts and books there. The museum is in a nice park. We often play in the park. I love that museum!

Tick the reasons why the writer likes the museum.

- There are old things and photos.
- There is a nice shop.
- He can "hear" the old things.
- He can do sports.

Look, choose and write.

There is a ______. We ______ there.

（hospital　park　playground　shop）

（do sports　go shopping　see a doctor　take a walk）

Write about a place in your community.

There is a ______. I/We ______ there.

### Project: Make a dream community model

Binbin and Amy are talking about their dream communities. Listen, tick and match.

（bus stop　hospital　park　playground　school　shop）

What is in your dream community? Talk and draw.

There is a shop in our dream community. People can go shopping there.

There is a playground. Children can have fun there.

Make a model of your dream community.

（配图：梦想社区模型，有 hospital、shop、park、school 等建筑）

Present your community model in class.

This is my dream community. There is ... People often ...

There are ... People often ...
