## Part B How can young people help their community?

### Let's talk

（配图：学生们在敬老院里帮忙）

Look! The students are busy now!

Chen Jie is making the bed.

Here is Zhang Peng. He's singing old songs.

Binbin and John are telling funny stories.

Everyone is happy!

### Look and say

What are the students doing for their community?

Look! The students are in an old people's home. Some are cleaning the room. Some ...

### Let's learn

She is making the bed.

He is singing old songs.

He is telling a funny story.

They are cleaning the room.

We're in an old people's home. I'm making the bed.

### Make fun sentences

（配图：两个转盘，转动词语组句）

- A cook is singing at the bus stop.
- A police officer is cooking at the children's centre.
- A cleaner is making the bed in the old people's home.
- A cook is telling a story in the park.

A cook ... ... in the park.

### Read and write

Read and match.

We are community helpers. We often help children and old people. In the kindergarten, John and Class One are singing songs together. Zhang Peng is telling stories to Class Two. In the old people's home, Chen Jie is making the bed. Amy is drawing a picture of Ms Xu. They are all happy. We love the people in our community.

- Amy — is — singing songs.
- Chen Jie — is — telling stories.
- Zhang Peng — is — making the bed.
- John and Class One — are — drawing a picture.

Look, choose and write.

John is ______ with Class One.

（cleaner　bus driver　cook　teacher）

（cleaning the rooms　singing songs　telling stories）

Choose a picture and complete the sentences.

He/She is a ______. He/She is ______ now.

### Project: Make a poster of your school helpers

The children are talking about jobs. Listen, tick and match.

（cleaner　cook　delivery worker　doctor　driver　police officer　teacher）

These four people do important jobs. Read and choose.

- A. Liu Jia's father takes us to school every day. Look! He is driving our school bus.
- B. Miss Lin often helps us. She is helping some students cross the road.
- C. Mr Zhang cleans our school. Look! He is cleaning the playground now.
- D. Ms Lu cooks nice food. She is cooking our lunch now.

Make your poster.

（配图：SCHOOL Workers & Helpers 海报模板）

- Mr Zhang is a cleaner. He cleans our school. Look! He is cleaning the playground now.
- ______ is a/an ______. He/She ______. Look! He/She is ______ now.

Share your poster in class.

Our school needs many helpers. Mr Zhang is a cleaner. He cleans our school. He's ...

**Self-check**

1. I can talk about people in my community and their jobs.
2. I can tell how people help in a community.
3. I can share how young people can help in their community.
4. I can read and spell words with "ph".

### Reading time

（配图：警察叔叔在给孩子们讲社区安全课）

Hello, boys and girls! Let's talk about safety in our community.

There is a big lake in the park. My family often take a walk there.

That's nice! But don't swim there. It's not safe.

Yes, sir!

What's wrong here? The light is red! But they're crossing the road.

Right! Don't cross at a red light!

Who can help her?

We can!

Yes! You can all help old people.

What do you see?

A big boy is helping a small boy.

We should help each other.

Right! Be nice to each other. Don't fight or hurt others.

Remember, stay safe and help others in our community!
