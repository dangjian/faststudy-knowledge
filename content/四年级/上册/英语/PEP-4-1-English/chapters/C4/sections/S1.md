## Part A What jobs are important in a community?

（配图：单元主题图，爸爸和儿子在社区花园里种花浇水）How can we help our community?

**Look and think**

- What are the people doing?
- What jobs are important in a community?

（配图：社区里人们在劳动的场景）

**Listen and chant**

What do people do,
To help the community?
Some wash the windows.
Some help others cross the street.

What can you do,
To help your community?
I can sing for old people,
Or tell stories for kids!

**Listen and sing**

Helping in the community

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们看到邻居消防员）

Look! Our neighbour is a firefighter. He often helps people.

A firefighter! Yes, he's a very nice man.

Liu Jia's father is also very nice.

Why?

He's a school bus driver. He takes us to school every day.

That's an important job too!

### Share and say

（配图：两个职业人物配图和句型）

He's a cleaner. He cleans the community.

She's a nurse. She helps people.

### Let's learn

COMMUNITY Helpers

- cleaner
- delivery worker
- cook
- bus driver
- police officer

（配图：社区工作者词汇配图）

My neighbour is a police officer. She helps a lot of people.

That's great!

### Match, act and guess

（bus driver　cleaner　cook　police officer　delivery worker）

You're a cleaner.

### Let's spell

Listen, repeat and chant.

**ph**

Philip has a phone.
He takes photos with his phone.
This is a photo of some elephants.
It's a photo from his phone!

Read, listen and number.

Philip　elephant　phone　photo

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

There are some elephants in the photo.

There are three elephants in the photo.

— By Philip
