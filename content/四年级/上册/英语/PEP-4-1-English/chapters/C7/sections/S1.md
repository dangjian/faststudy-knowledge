## Read aloud and act it out.

（配图：孩子们在教室里讨论社区服务计划）

**Let's help!**

Class, how can you help your community? What can you do?

We can go to the park and give out some posters.

We can help the cleaners.

We can also water the plants.

Sounds great!

## Listen and finish the plans.

Place to go: ______

Place to go: the children's centre

Things to do: ______

（bus stop　hospital　old people's home　park　playground　shop）

（clean the room　cook　do some chores　do sports　go shopping　play games　take a walk）

Which idea do you like? What's your plan?
