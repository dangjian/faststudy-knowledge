# Revision Let's help!

## Read aloud and act it out.

（配图：孩子们在教室里讨论社区服务计划）

**Let's help!**

Class, how can you help your community? What can you do?

We can go to the park and give out some posters.

We can help the cleaners.

We can also water the plants.

Sounds great!

## Listen and finish the plans.

Place to go: ______

Place to go: the children's centre

Things to do: ______

（bus stop　hospital　old people's home　park　playground　shop）

（clean the room　cook　do some chores　do sports　go shopping　play games　take a walk）

Which idea do you like? What's your plan?

## Read, write and talk.

**Let's help!**

I'm Wu Binbin. My family often helps at the children's centre. My dad is a doctor. He is kind. He often helps the children there. My mum is a cook. She cooks nice food there. My uncle is a PE teacher. He plays football with the children. My sister Xinxin and I play games with them. All my family help make our community better.

What do Binbin's family members do at the children's centre?

| Family member | Job | What do they often do? |
|------|------|------|
| Binbin and Xinxin |  | They play games with the children. |
| Binbin's father |  | He often ______ the children. |
| Binbin's mother |  | She ______ nice food there. |
| Binbin's uncle |  | He ______ with the children. |

How does your family help your community?
