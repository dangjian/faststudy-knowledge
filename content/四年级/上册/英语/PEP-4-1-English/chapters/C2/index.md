# Unit 2 My friends

（配图：单元主题图，一群孩子勾肩搭背开心大笑）What makes your friends different?

---

## Part A Who are your friends?

**Look and think**

- How are these children different?
- How are your friends different?

（配图：外形各不相同的孩子）

**Listen and chant**

Some friends are short.
Some friends are tall.
My friends are special.
I love them all.

Some friends' hair is short.
Some friends' hair is long.
My friends are great.
We all get along.

**Listen and sing**

My friends

### Let's talk

（配图：迈克和妈妈谈论新朋友）

Mum, I have a new friend.

Really? What's your friend's name?

His name is Zhang Peng. Look! He's tall and strong. He has nice short hair too.

Yes. He's also kind. He often helps me.

### Say and draw

He's tall and strong. He has short hair.

Let me see. He's tall and strong. I know! It's Zhang Peng.

### Let's learn

- short hair
- tall and strong
- kind
- long hair
- short and thin
- quiet

（配图：六组人物特征配图）

I have a friend. She has long hair and ______. Who is she?

Her name is Sarah.

**Listen and chant**

Tim is my friend.
He's a good, good boy.
He's tall and thin.
He's a good, good boy.

Lily is my friend.
She's a good, good girl.
She's quiet and kind.
She's a good, good girl.

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**sh**

I have no shell. / Can we share? / Share? No, we can't.

You can share my shell! / Shoo! Go away, fish!

Read, listen and number.

English　share　shop　she　fish　ship

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

True friends, tall or strong or not, they help each other.

## Part B How do we choose our friends?

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们谈论自己最好的朋友）

Who's your best friend?

Chen Jie. She's funny. She often makes me smile. How about you?

My best friend is John. He's very kind. He often helps me with English.

### Talk about your best friend

（配图：思维导图句型支架）

- is：tall / thin / long hair
- often：draws pictures with me / helps me
- has：long hair

Who's your best friend?

Liu Jia. She's tall and ... She has long hair. She often ...

### Let's learn

Who's your best friend?

Zhang Peng.

He often reads books with me.

- He reads books with me.
- He helps me with Chinese.
- We play games together.
- We play football together.

### Look, choose and say

I like my friend. My friend likes me.

- I read books. → My friend read/reads books with me.
- I play games. → My friend play/plays games with me.
- I play football. → My friend play/plays football with me.
- I help my friend with Chinese. → My friend help/helps me with English.

### Read and write

Read and compare.

**My best friend**

Liu Jia is tall. She has long hair. She often reads books with me. We like animal books and often share. She likes weiqi, but I like ball games. I often play football and basketball. Liu Jia is kind. She always makes me smile. We are best friends.

Put the numbers in the correct places.

1. like animal books
2. like ball games
3. like weiqi
4. read books
5. share

Choose and write.

Liu Jia is ______. She has ______ hair. She often ______.

（long　kind　short　strong　tall）

（helps me with ...　plays football　plays games　reads books）

What about your best friend?

- He/She is ______.
- He/She has ______ hair.
- He/She often ______.
