## Part A How are families different?

（配图：单元主题图，哥哥和妹妹一起提着洗衣篮，妈妈端着水杯站在厨房旁）What do family do together?

**Look and think**

- How do these children help at home?
- How do you help at home?

（配图：孩子们在家做家务的场景）

**Listen and chant**

Can you help?
Yes, I can. I can clean my room.
Can she help?
Yes, she can. She can do some chores.
Can he help?
Yes, he can. He can sweep the floor.
Can we help?
Yes, we can. That's for sure!

**Listen and sing**

Helping at home

### Let's talk

（配图：迈克和张鹏看全家福照片）

You have a big family. Is this your father?

Yes, it is. He's a PE teacher.

What's your mother's job?

She's a doctor.

Doctors are great! My father is a doctor too.

### Draw and say

What's your mother's job? — She's a teacher.

### Let's learn

They are helpful!

- farmer
- nurse
- doctor
- office worker
- factory worker

（配图：五种职业人物配图）

What's your father's job? — He's a factory worker.

### Match and say

What's Mike's job? — He's a doctor.

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**ch**

My grandpa can teach Chinese. He can make nice chairs.

My grandma can make good lunches. Her peaches are great!

Read, listen and circle.

lunch　China　chair　kitchen　Chinese　much　child　teacher

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

My grandpa is a Chinese teacher.

People speak Chinese in China.
