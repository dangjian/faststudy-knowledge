# Unit 1 Helping at home

## Part A How are families different?

**Look and think**

- How do these children help at home?
- How do you help at home?

（配图：孩子们在家做家务的场景）

**Listen and chant**

Can you help?
Yes, I can. I can clean my room.
Can she help?
Yes, she can. She can do some chores.
Can he help?
Yes, he can. He can sweep the floor.
Can we help?
Yes, we can. That's for sure!

**Listen and sing**

Helping at home

### Let's talk

（配图：迈克和张鹏看全家福照片）

You have a big family. Is this your father?

Yes, it is. He's a PE teacher.

What's your mother's job?

She's a doctor.

Doctors are great! My father is a doctor too.

### Draw and say

They are helpful!

- farmer
- nurse
- doctor
- office worker
- factory worker

（配图：五种职业人物配图）

### Match and say

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**ch**

My grandma can make good lunches. Her peaches are great!

Read, listen and circle.

lunch　China　chair　kitchen　Chinese　much　child　teacher

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

## Part B How can we help our family?

### Let's talk

（配图：迈克和妈妈在家，爸爸妈妈又忙又累）

Mum and Dad are busy and tired. What can we do for them?

We can do some chores.

I can cook.

I can clean the room.

We can also make a gift!

### Make a list and talk

（配图：重阳节主题图，孩子为爷爷奶奶做事的清单）

- sing
- clean the room
- do some chores
- draw a picture

The Double Ninth Festival is coming. What can we do for our grandparents?

I can ...

### Let's learn

Be happy together

I look after my sister. We sweep the floor.

We cook together. We are happy together.

I have a happy family.

（配图：一家人一起做家务的场景）We often cook together and ...

**Listen and chant**

Mum and Dad are busy.
I cook for my family.
I look after my doggy.
I sweep the floor. It's easy.
We are a happy family.

### Read and write

Read and tick.

（配图：两张留言卡和妈妈的话）

Mum, I am a big boy now. I can sweep the floor. I can cook. I can look after my baby sister too!
— Xiao Lin

Mum, you are a great nurse. You help many people. I can help you at home!
— Xiao Lin

Xiao Lin, you are still a child. What can you do?
— Mum

Choose and write.

What's the mother's job?

She is a ______.

（factory worker　farmer　doctor　nurse　office worker）

What can the boy do?

He can ______ the floor.

He can ______ his sister.

（cook　look after　sweep）

How can you help your family?

I can ...
