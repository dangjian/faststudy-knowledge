# Unit 6 Changing for the seasons

（配图：单元主题图，三个孩子穿着冬装在雪地里堆雪人）How are our lives different in each season?

---

## Part A What do we wear in different seasons?

**Look and think**

- What are these children doing?
- Do you like this season? Why?

（配图：孩子们玩雪的场景）

**Listen and chant**

Spring, summer, autumn and winter.
Warm, hot, cool and cold.
Spring, summer, autumn and winter.
What clothes can we wear?
In spring, wear a sweater.
In summer, wear shorts.
In autumn, wear a jacket.
In winter, wear a lot!

**Listen and sing**

What should I wear today?

### Let's talk

（配图：衣帽间里，孩子和妈妈找衣服）

Whose sweater is this, Mum?

It's your dad's. Put it there, please.

Can I wear this new shirt today?

Yes, but wear a coat too. It's cold and windy outside.

Whose socks are these?

They're mine.

### Match, ask and answer

（配图：三件衣服标注气温 23℃、13℃、7℃）

Whose sweater is it? — It's Lu Wei's.

Whose shirts are these? — They're Xiao Fei's.

- sweater
- socks
- shirt
- dress

## Part B What do we do in different seasons?

### Let's talk

（配图：孩子们聊喜欢的季节）

Sarah, which season do you like?

Winter. It snows a lot. I can play in the snow and make a snowman. What about you?

I like winter too. There are many festivals. My family and I often get together and have fun.

### Act and guess

Which season do you like? Guess.

You like winter.

You're right!

### Let's learn

- spring
- summer
- autumn
- winter

（配图：四季词汇配图）

Which season do you like?

Winter. I can make a snowman.

### Do a survey

（配图：条形统计图，纵轴 students（40—0），横轴 spring、summer、autumn、winter）

Which season do you like?

Thirty students like spring. Fifteen students like ...

### Read and write

Read and write F (Fact) or O (Opinion).

**Colourful seasons, colourful life!**

Spring is green. It's full of life. I play games and sing songs, and fly my kite.

Summer is blue. I take many walks, wear dresses and T-shirts. The days are so long.

Autumn is orange. I like the falling leaves. I wear sweaters and shirts, and enjoy mooncakes.

Winter is white. I make snowmen with friends, wear warm coats and gloves. Then spring comes again.

1. Summer is blue.（　）
2. I play games and sing songs.（　）
3. I like the falling leaves.（　）
4. Spring comes after winter.（　）

Look, choose and write.

Whose ______ is it? — It's mine.

（dress　shirt　coat　sweater）

Which season do you like? Why?

I like ______. I can ______.

（autumn　spring　summer　winter）

（fly a kite　make a snowman　take a walk）

### Project: Make a travel card

The children are talking about seasons. Listen, tick and match.

（winter　autumn　summer　spring）

Which are Mike's clothes? Which are Chen Jie's? Look and choose.

The green sweater is Mike's. The yellow dress is Chen Jie's.

（Mike's / Chen Jie's：dress　shirt　socks　coat　cap　sweater）

Talk in groups.

- I can wear my new T-shirt.
- Yes, it's hot there.
- We can swim in the sea.
- I can eat seafood.
- I can go to Haikou in winter.

Make a travel card. Then share it in class.

（配图：travel card 模板）

- I like ______.
- It's ______.
- I can go to ______.
- I can wear ______.
- I can eat ______.

I like spring. It's warm and windy. I can go to the park and fly kites. I can wear my pink sweater and ...

**Self-check**

1. I can talk about what people wear in different seasons.
2. I can tell people about my favourite season.
3. I can talk about how seasons change our daily lives.
4. I can read and spell words with "wh".

### Reading time

（配图：圣诞节，来自澳大利亚的孩子和朋友聊天）

Welcome! Merry Christmas!

Thank you!

Where is your coat? It's very cold outside.

I don't have a coat, but I have a warm sweater.

What do you usually do on Christmas Day?

I put on my shorts and T-shirt and go to the beach.

You can go to the beach on Christmas Day?

Yes, but it's cool at night. I need to wear a sweater.

Brrr! It's cold and snowy. You need a warm hat.

Yes, I have one. Thank you. OK. Let's go.

Oh look! It's a snowman. He looks cold. He needs a warm hat.

Yes, it looks like you. Ha! Ha! Snowmen love cold weather.
