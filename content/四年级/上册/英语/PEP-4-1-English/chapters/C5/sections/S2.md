## Part B What do we do in different weather?

### Let's talk

（配图：窗外下雨，约翰和妈妈商量去哪儿玩）

John, it's raining now.

Oh no! We can't play basketball in the park.

It's OK. We can go to the library.

But it's closed now.

Hmm. Let's go and see a film.

That's a good idea!

### Choose and say

（warm and sunny　warm and windy　cold and rainy　cold and windy　hot and sunny）

It's cold and rainy. We can't play football. But we can make cards at home.

### Let's learn

- play basketball
- fly a kite
- see a film
- make a snowman

On sunny days, we can play basketball.

On snowy days, we can make a snowman.

### Play a wheel game

（配图：天气词与活动短语组成的词轮：sunny、cloudy、windy、rainy、snowy / fly a kite、make a card、make a snowman、go to the park、play football、see a film）

It's cold and snowy. We can't fly a kite.

What can we do?

We can ...

### Read and write

Read and answer.

（配图：明信片，正面是悉尼公园）

Hi John,

How are you? I'm in a big park in Sydney today. It's hot and sunny here. It's fun to go to the park with my new friends. We play basketball together. Their children swim in the pool. How about you? Is it cold and windy in Beijing? Can you fly your kite?

Love,
Dad

（Dongdajie, Beijing 10****, P. R. China / Carl Baker, *** Bridge St. Sydney NSW 2000 Australia）

1. What's the weather like in Sydney?
2. What can John's father do there?

Choose and write.

POSTCARD

Hi, Dad!

I'm doing fine in Beijing.

It's ______ here. I can ______.

Love,
John

（cloudy　rainy　sunny　snowy　windy）

（fly a kite　play basketball　make a snowman　read books　see a film）

### Project: Make a bad weather reminder

Sort the weather from good to bad. Then listen and circle.

（配图：Good—Bad 天气分类条）

What can you do in different weather? Think, write and say.

- Good weather：It is ... We can ...
- Bad weather：There is ... We can't ...

（cool and cloudy　strong wind　warm and windy　heavy rain　cold and snowy　heavy snow）

It's cool and cloudy. We can play basketball.

There is heavy rain now. We can't play basketball. But we can read some books.

Make a weather reminder.

（配图：Bad weather reminder 提示牌模板）

It's too hot! We can't play football outside.

Share your reminder in class.

It's too hot. We can't play football outside. There is a strong wind. We can't ...

**Self-check**

1. I can talk about different weather.
2. I can say what my friends and I do in different weather.
3. I can tell others how people can have fun in different weather.
4. I can read and spell words with "th".

### Reading time

（配图：拟人化的"雨"和妈妈聊天，窗外孩子们在不同天气里玩耍）

Mum, do the children like me?

Of course! Why?

Look, it's sunny! They're playing football on the grass! They look happy!

Look, it's windy! The boy is flying a kite and smiling.

Yes. He's having fun.

Look, it's snowy! They can play in the snow.

Yes, they're making a snowman and having a snowball fight.

Now it's rainy! That girl is not happy.

They can play in the rain, dear! It's rainy and cool. We can wear raincoats and boots. Let's go out and dance in the rain.

That sounds fun!

They like me!
