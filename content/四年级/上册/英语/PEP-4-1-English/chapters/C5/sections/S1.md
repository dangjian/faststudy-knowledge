## Part A How can the weather be different?

（配图：单元主题图，一家人在紫色花田里奔跑放风筝）How can the weather change our lives?

**Look and think**

- What's the weather like in the picture?
- What can you do in this weather?

（配图：不同天气的场景）

**Listen and chant**

It's sunny today.
I do sports and I play.
It's windy today.
I fly a kite and I play.
It's snowy today.
I make a snowman and I play.
It's rainy today.
I stay at home and I wait.

**Listen and sing**

The weather and us

### Let's talk

（配图：约翰给悉尼的马克打电话）

Hello! Mark speaking.

Hi, Mark! This is John. What's the weather like in Sydney?

Well, it's sunny today.

Is it hot?

No, it isn't. It's 21 degrees.

That's not bad. It's only two degrees in Beijing.

Wow! It's cold!

### Look and say

（配图：世界城市气温图，标注 Beijing、London、Toronto、Sydney、Singapore、Moscow 的气温）

What's the weather like in Beijing?

It's cool and sunny.

### Let's learn

- windy
- cloudy
- sunny
- rainy
- snowy

（配图：天气词汇配图）

- cold
- hot
- warm
- cool

（配图：温度计配图）

What's the weather like in Beijing tomorrow?

It's cool and sunny.

How about Sydney?

It's warm and windy.

### Look, ask and answer

（配图：地图上标注 Harbin、Guilin、Wuhan、Guangzhou）

What's the weather like in Harbin?

It's cold and snowy. The boy is having fun in the snow.

### Let's spell

Listen and repeat. Then read aloud.

**th**

It's warm again. Good to see you!

I'm thirsty and hungry. You are so thin now.

These gifts are for you.

Thanks. They're my favourite!

Read, listen and circle.

these　there　thank　them　think　they　three　thin

Look, listen and write.

（配图：看图听音写句子）

Mum, you're very kind now.

Thank you, my dear!
