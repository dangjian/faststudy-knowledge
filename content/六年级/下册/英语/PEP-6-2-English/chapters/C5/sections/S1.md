## Day 1-4

### Day 1 A beautiful farm

（配图：美丽的农场，松鼠举着 a / an / some 提示牌）

Mike visited a farm. What can you see on the farm?

______ pear　　______ jacket　　______ egg　　______ orange

______ umbrella　　______ apples　　______ horses　　______ tomatoes

（配图：农场场景，桌上有水果，栅栏后有羊、奶牛、马，椅子上搭着外套和雨伞）

- What else can you see?

Can you name the groups of words?

| Fruit | | | | | |
|-------|--|--|--|--|--|
| apple | egg | jacket | horse | tomato | |
| orange | cake | shoes | | | |

Can you think of one more word group? Can you add more words to the groups?

### Day 2 Farmers' market

Mrs Booth and Bill want to go shopping. Help them finish their mind map.

（配图：思维导图，中心为 farmers' market，分支：When?（morning）/ Where? / With who? / How can we get there? / How much? / shopping list）

Now make a new word map with your partner.

Mike met new friends. Help him talk about them.

（配图：Mrs Booth 和 Bill 的人物特征图：Bill—short、tall；Mrs Booth—thin、young、long hair、a red bag、white shoes）

Bill is my new friend. He's short and heavy.

### Day 3 Life on the farm

These are things people often do on the farm. Circle the words in the puzzle. Write down what he, she or they do every day.

（配图：单词寻宝游戏，字母串：pqswimmingbdcboatingarthomeworkc / gsoubasketballpipawdisheszping-pong）

- He goes fishing.
- She does the cleaning.
- He plays music.
- They have some juice.

These people are having fun on the farm. Write down three sentences about what they are doing.

Bill is playing ping-pong.

### Day 4 Mike's day on the farm

What did Mike do on the farm?

Saturday was a busy day for me. In the morning, I got up early. Then I ______. After that, I ______. Next, I ______. In the afternoon, I ______. In the evening, I ______. Finally, I ______ at 10 p.m.

Can you retell what Mike did on Saturday?

Now read about Mike and Bill's game.

There are three pictures.

1. Show one picture.

Bill: It's an elephant. It's big.

2. Get another picture, but don't turn it over.

Bill: Guess! Is this bigger or smaller than the elephant?

Mike: I think it's bigger than the elephant.

Bill: No, it isn't. It's smaller. Look. It's a tiger.

3. Show the last picture.

Bill: Look. The other card is a whale.

Mike: So the whale is the biggest, and the tiger is the smallest.
