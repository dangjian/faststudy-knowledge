## Part A

### 单元主情景图

（配图：展览馆里挂着一张旧合影，Wu Binbin 和爷爷、爸爸在参观，讲解员在介绍）

- Binbin, that's your father.
- Wow!
- You weren't very big then, Mr Wu!
- No, so I didn't like PE class very much. I couldn't run fast or play basketball well.

### 主情景对话

**Then and now**

（配图：学校新老照片对比场景）

- Our school was small! We didn't have a library, and there weren't any computer rooms at all.
- There was no gym either!

### Let's try

Today, there is a guest visiting the school. Listen and tick or cross.

1. The school changed a little.
2. Grandpa could use computers when he was at school.

### Let's talk

Grandpa: Wow, a library! There was no library in my old school.

Sarah: Tell us about your school, please.

Grandpa: There was only one small building on a hill.

Wu Binbin: Could you see stars at night?

Grandpa: Yes, I liked the stars.

Sarah: Me too. One day I'm going to visit the moon.

Wu Binbin: The Americans took about five days to get there in 1969.

Sarah: How do you know that?

Wu Binbin: Easy, I looked it up on the Internet.

Grandpa: Ah! There were no computers or Internet in my time.

- What was Grandpa's school like?

What was your school like five years ago? What is it like now? Draw and talk.

（配图：五年前与现在的学校对比画框（five years ago / now），提示句型：There was ... / There is ... / There were ... / There are ...）

### Let's learn

（配图：学校新旧对比照片，词汇：dining hall / gym / grass；时间：... years ago / ... months ago / last year / last month）

Wu Binbin's father: There was no gym in my school thirty years ago.

Wu Binbin: Now there's a new one in our school.

### Find the mistakes

In the Tang dynasty ...

At that time, there were no gyms. People didn't go by bus. People couldn't use the Internet.
