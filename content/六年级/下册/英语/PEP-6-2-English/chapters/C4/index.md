# Unit 4 Then and now


## Part A

### 单元主情景图

（配图：展览馆里挂着一张旧合影，Wu Binbin 和爷爷、爸爸在参观，讲解员在介绍）

- Binbin, that's your father.
- Wow!
- You weren't very big then, Mr Wu!
- No, so I didn't like PE class very much. I couldn't run fast or play basketball well.

### 主情景对话

**Then and now**

（配图：学校新老照片对比场景）

- Our school was small! We didn't have a library, and there weren't any computer rooms at all.
- There was no gym either!

### Let's try

Today, there is a guest visiting the school. Listen and tick or cross.

1. The school changed a little.
2. Grandpa could use computers when he was at school.

### Let's talk

Grandpa: Wow, a library! There was no library in my old school.

Sarah: Tell us about your school, please.

Grandpa: There was only one small building on a hill.

Wu Binbin: Could you see stars at night?

Grandpa: Yes, I liked the stars.

Sarah: Me too. One day I'm going to visit the moon.

Wu Binbin: The Americans took about five days to get there in 1969.

Sarah: How do you know that?

Wu Binbin: Easy, I looked it up on the Internet.

Grandpa: Ah! There were no computers or Internet in my time.

- What was Grandpa's school like?

What was your school like five years ago? What is it like now? Draw and talk.

（配图：五年前与现在的学校对比画框（five years ago / now），提示句型：There was ... / There is ... / There were ... / There are ...）

### Let's learn

（配图：学校新旧对比照片，词汇：dining hall / gym / grass；时间：... years ago / ... months ago / last year / last month）

Wu Binbin's father: There was no gym in my school thirty years ago.

Wu Binbin: Now there's a new one in our school.

### Find the mistakes

In the Tang dynasty ...

At that time, there were no gyms. People didn't go by bus. People couldn't use the Internet.

## Part B

### Let's try

Mike's friends are visiting his home. Listen and circle.

1. What are they talking about?
   A. Mike's hobby.　　B. Mike's old photos.
2. What grade was Mike in?
   A. Grade 1.　　B. Grade 5.

### Let's talk

Chen Jie: John, come and look at Mike's old photos!

John: That's funny, Mike! You're wearing a pink T-shirt.

Mike: Yes, but I don't like pink now.

Chen Jie: We are all different now!

Mike: Right. Before, I was quiet. Now, I'm very active in class. How about you?

John: Well, I was short, so I couldn't ride my bike well. Now, I go cycling every day. I like it very much.

Chen Jie: That's good exercise.

- How did Mike change?

Talk about your friends or family members. How did they change?

wasn't tall ...　　didn't like maths / ...　　had long ... hair

didn't wear glasses ...　　didn't like cats ...　　didn't like eggs / ...

### Let's learn

（配图：运动活动图片，词汇：go cycling / ice-skate / play badminton）

Wu Binbin: I didn't like winter before. I thought it was too cold, and I couldn't go cycling.

Mike: How about now?

Wu Binbin: Now I love to ice-skate, so I like winter.

### Read and write

Tell your partner.

1. What animal can run very fast?
2. What makes you feel worried?

**What a dream!**

（配图：Wu Binbin 梦中赛跑的连环画）

Wu Binbin had a race with his father and Max. There were many people there. His father ran very fast, but Wu Binbin could not. There was nothing he could do. He was so worried. "I could run fast at school," he thought. "Why am I so slow now?"

Then Robin said, "Drink this!" and gave Wu Binbin some water. Wu Binbin drank it and suddenly he felt good. He could run fast again.

There was a second race. There were many animals in that race. Wu Binbin ran like a cheetah. He could win the race! But suddenly he tripped and fell. Then he woke up. He was in bed. It was all a dream!

Number the pictures.

Wu Binbin is telling his mother about the dream. Listen and fill in the blanks.

Wu Binbin: Mum, I ______ an interesting dream last night.

Mum: What did you dream about?

Wu Binbin: I had a race with Dad and Max, but I ______.

Mum: What happened then?

Wu Binbin: Robin ______ me some water. I ______ it and then could run very fast. Suddenly I tripped and fell. Then I woke up.

Mum: What a dream!

Did you have an interesting dream? Tell your dream to your partner. Then write several sentences about it.

I had a dream last night. In my dream, I ______.

What a dream!

### Story time

（配图：三个时代对比：原始人生活、现在、2050 年的家务机器人）

- Cavemen often ate raw meat. They didn't cook their food.
- Now there are many things to help you cook.
- In 2050, there will be house robots. They will cook, clean and wash the dishes.

Zip: Oh, cavemen didn't cook their meat?

Zoom: It's OK. They didn't do the dishes either!

- There were many trees, and there were many clean rivers.
- Now, there are more and more buildings.
- We should help the earth now, so birds will have homes and the forests will be healthy and green again.

Zip: Look! How beautiful the world was! Oh! What will the birds do?

Zoom: Don't worry. People will help them.

- Before humans lived on the earth, whales already lived in the sea.
- Now, there are not many whales.
- If we change now, whales and humans will have a bright future.

Zip: Heal the world!

Zoom: Make it a better place!
