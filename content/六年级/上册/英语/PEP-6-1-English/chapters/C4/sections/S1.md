## Part A Why do we use money?

### Let's talk

Mum is giving Matt his pocket money. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. Matt gets 25 yuan for his piggy bank. (　)
2. Matt is going to buy some pencils. (　)

Mike: Are you counting your pocket money, Matt?
Matt: Yes. I have three hundred and fifty-seven yuan.
Mike: Wow, that's a lot! How are you going to spend it?
Matt: I'm going to buy a schoolbag.
Mike: Yes, you need a new one.
Matt: I also want to buy some animal books.
Mike: Nice. They're interesting.
Matt: Maybe I can also buy some ice cream.
Mike: Sure, but don't eat too much.
Matt: Don't worry. I won't.

- How much pocket money does Matt have?
- How is he going to spend his pocket money?

### Let's learn

（配图：购物与服务业插图）

People use money to do different things.

We use money to buy goods. We buy food and drink. We buy clothes and school things.

We use money to pay for services. We need different services: haircuts, home deliveries, ...

Chen Jie: How are you going to spend your pocket money?
Amy: I'm going to have a haircut, ...
