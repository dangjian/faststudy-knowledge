## Part A Where do we get energy?

### Let's talk

The children are having a science class. Listen and answer.

1. What was the class about?
2. What is their homework?

Binbin: Hi, Mike. Any ideas about our project?
Mike: Maybe we can talk about water power.
Binbin: OK. We use water every day. Let's find out more about water power.
Mike: What do you know about it?
Binbin: Running water can make electricity. We can get power from the sea too.
Mike: What else do you want to know about water?
Binbin: Well, where does water come from? How do we make power from water?

Binbin: Good idea!

（配图：风能、太阳能、水能插图）

We use green energy to light, heat and cool our homes and schools. We also use it to run cars, buses and trains.

## Part B How do we save energy?

### Let's talk

Binbin and Mike are presenting their project. Listen and answer.

1. What makes some rivers and lakes dry up?
2. Do Binbin and Mike think it's too late to change? Why?

Mrs Green: We use energy every day. But many resources are running out. What should we do?
Mike: We should save energy.
Mrs Green: Yes, but how can we save energy?
Binbin: We can change our energy use.
Mrs Green: Can you give us an example, Binbin?
Binbin: Sure. We can take quick showers. That will save energy and water.
Mike: I agree. We can also use LED lighting.
Binbin: And we should always unplug our computers and TVs.
Mrs Green: Great! Small changes can make a big difference.

- Why do we have to save energy?
- What did Binbin and Mike suggest?

### Let's learn

（配图：3R 图标：Reduce / Reuse / Recycle）

- Reduce: Use air conditioners less. Walk more and drive less. Take quick showers.
- Reuse: Bring our own bags to shops and markets.
- Recycle: Recycle bottles and boxes. Recycle old clothes, toys and books.

Let's follow the "3R" rules. We should use air conditioners less. We should ...

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

**Save energy, save our Earth!**

Mother Earth is running out of resources! We should all try to ______ three ideas.

**Idea 1**

Useful tips to save water:

- Take a quick shower.
- Wash all your dirty clothes together, not one at a time.
- Wash fruit and vegetables, then use the water again.

Think: How does a quick shower save energy?

**Idea 2**

Useful tips to use less electricity:

- Use LED lighting.
- Turn off your TV or computer and unplug it.
- Set your air conditioner to 26°C or more in summer.

Think: How can you use air conditioners less?

**Idea 3**

Great tips to save and reuse resources:

- Use both sides of paper.
- Reuse old bottles. You can put pencils in them.

Think: What are your ideas for following the "3R" rules?

### Reading time

（配图：爷爷、Binbin 和 Robin 在户外的插图，12:00）

It was a sunny day. Grandpa, Binbin and Robin played outside. They didn't want to go back to the kitchen to cook. So, what could they do?

Grandpa had an idea. "The sun is very hot. We can use solar power to make food here," he said. "Let's make a solar 'oven'!"

（配图：制作太阳能烤箱的插图）

"Wow! How does the box keep the heat? Why do we use black paper?" asked Binbin.

Soon their food was ready.

"This looks delicious! And we cooked with no gas or electricity! That's super cool!" said Binbin.

"Sunlight is a great source of energy. It can help light our house. It can run cars. And we can use it to cook!" said Robin.

"Yes, we should use energy in smart ways," said Grandpa with a smile.
