## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

**Save energy, save our Earth!**

Mother Earth is running out of resources! We should all try to ______ three ideas.

**Idea 1**

Useful tips to save water:

- Take a quick shower.
- Wash all your dirty clothes together, not one at a time.
- Wash fruit and vegetables, then use the water again.

Think: How does a quick shower save energy?

**Idea 2**

Useful tips to use less electricity:

- Use LED lighting.
- Turn off your TV or computer and unplug it.
- Set your air conditioner to 26°C or more in summer.

Think: How can you use air conditioners less?

**Idea 3**

Great tips to save and reuse resources:

- Use both sides of paper.
- Reuse old bottles. You can put pencils in them.

Think: What are your ideas for following the "3R" rules?

Choose the best title for each idea.

- Idea 1 (　)　A. Do small things to save water.
- Idea 2 (　)　B. Save and reuse resources.
- Idea 3 (　)　C. Save electricity.

Answer the questions at the end of each idea. Then share with the class.

Sincerely,
The Editor

- Write an interesting opening. At home, what things use energy? Where does the energy come from?

### Reading time

（配图：爷爷、Binbin 和 Robin 在户外的插图，12:00）

It was a sunny day. Grandpa, Binbin and Robin played outside. They didn't want to go back to the kitchen to cook. So, what could they do?

Grandpa had an idea. "The sun is very hot. We can use solar power to make food here," he said. "Let's make a solar 'oven'!"

（配图：制作太阳能烤箱的插图）

"Wow! How does the box keep the heat? Why do we use black paper?" asked Binbin.

Soon their food was ready.

"This looks delicious! And we cooked with no gas or electricity! That's super cool!" said Binbin.

"Sunlight is a great source of energy. It can help light our house. It can run cars. And we can use it to cook!" said Robin.

"Yes, we should use energy in smart ways," said Grandpa with a smile.
