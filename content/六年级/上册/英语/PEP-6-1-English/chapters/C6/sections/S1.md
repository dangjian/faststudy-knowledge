## Part A Where do we get energy?

### Let's talk

The children are having a science class. Listen and answer.

1. What was the class about?
2. What is their homework?

Binbin: Hi, Mike. Any ideas about our project?
Mike: Maybe we can talk about water power.
Binbin: OK. We use water every day. Let's find out more about water power.
Mike: What do you know about it?
Binbin: Running water can make electricity. We can get power from the sea too.
Mike: What else do you want to know about water?
Binbin: Well, where does water come from? How do we make power from water?
Mike: Good questions. Let's go to the library. We can work on it together.
Binbin: Good idea!

- What did they choose for their project?
- What does Binbin want to know about it?

### Let's learn

（配图：风能、太阳能、水能插图）

Wind power, solar power and water power are types of green energy. They are clean and good for Mother Earth.

We use green energy to light, heat and cool our homes and schools. We also use it to run cars, buses and trains.
