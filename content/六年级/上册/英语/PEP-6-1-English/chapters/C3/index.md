## Part A How do we know we are ill?

### Let's talk

Mike and Chen Jie are in the playground. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).

1. Mike has a runny nose. (　)
2. Mike should go home. (　)

Mike: Hello?
Binbin: Hi, Mike. It's Binbin. I'm going to the science museum tomorrow. Are you going?
Mike: I'm afraid not, Binbin. I have a bad cold.
Binbin: Oh dear! How do you feel?
Mike: I feel ill. My head hurts and I have a runny nose.
Binbin: I'm sorry to hear that. Maybe you should see a doctor.
Mike: I know. My mum is going to take me to hospital this afternoon.
Binbin: That's good. Take care and get well soon!
Mike: Thanks.

- How does Mike feel?
- What should Mike do?

Miss White: Class, now we're going to discuss health. First, what is health?
Chen Jie: Health is having a good body and feeling happy.
Miss White: Thank you, Chen Jie. So, how can we live a healthy life?

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Read the story. Why was Liu Jia feeling down?

Liu Jia was crying. So Miss White asked her, "What's the matter, Liu Jia? You look sad." Liu Jia answered, "I'm feeling down. My good friend Lucy moved to another city."

Miss White listened carefully and told Liu Jia, "Think about happy things. Call your friend or send her some emails."

"I tried that, but I still feel unhappy," said Liu Jia.

Miss White gave her a glass of water. "Maybe you can do some exercise. Exercise may cheer you up!"

"Join some after-school clubs and make new friends! You can talk and have fun with them," added Miss White.

"I will try. Thank you, Miss White," said Liu Jia.

Dear Miss White,

I often make video calls with my friend now. I also joined a ping-pong club and made some new friends. I feel much better. Thank you so much!

Liu Jia

### Reading time

（配图：双手对话插图，右撇子与左手）

**Helping hands**

My name is Right Hand. This is my twin brother, Left Hand.

One day, our master didn't wash us and ate his food. He felt ill the next day. Now he washes us before every meal.

On another day, our master had a bad cold. He had a runny nose and a cough. We wiped his nose and covered his mouth with a tissue. We're important for his health.

Happiness is important for good health. One day, our master felt unhappy, so we helped him make handicrafts (手工艺品). He felt happy again.

Our master also cooked food for his family. They thanked him and told him, "You have really great hands!"

We're important for our master and his friends. He uses us to give them a high five!

We really are helping hands!
