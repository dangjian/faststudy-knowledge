# Appendix 1 Songs（歌曲）

## Unit 1 Travelling around

I visited Beijing,
And I learnt about the place.
I went to Tian'anmen Square,
And to the Great Wall the next day.

I visited Beijing,
And I learnt about its history.
I took so many pictures,
And ate new snacks on the street.

I went to Xi'an next,
And I saw the old city wall.
I ate some really tasty noodles,
And sang a local song.

On my trip to Xi'an,
I saw the famous sights.
And I climbed up Lishan Mountain.
The views were very nice.

## Unit 2 Getting together

My family and I, we have a great time.
We get together at special events.
Events are fun. I love every one.
Yeah, yeah, yeah! Yeah, yeah, yeah!

My parents and I, we had a sports day.
There were many fun activities to do.
We ran so fast. My brother clapped.
Go, go, go! Go, go, go!

My mother and I, we cleaned up at New Year,
And pasted *fu* on our door.
We dressed in red. All were fed.
Yum, yum, yum! Yum, yum, yum!

My brother and I, we sang some songs,
And spoke with all of our relatives.
We counted down, such a big sound.
3, 2, 1! Fun, fun, fun!

## Unit 3 How can we stay healthy?

How can we stay healthy?
The boy has a runny nose.
He should see a doctor.
Maybe he knows.

How can we stay healthy?
The girl has a little cough.
She should get enough sleep.
Let's switch all the lights off.

How can we stay healthy?
We shouldn't feel so sad.
We should talk about our feelings.
Sharing isn't bad.

How can we stay healthy?
Don't feel lonely or alone.
Join after-school teams and clubs.
Have fun at school and at home.

## Unit 4 Did they use money well?

Simon bought a new computer.
He bought a new computer.
It was an expensive computer.
Did he use his money well?

Mary bought two schoolbags.
She bought two schoolbags.
Now Mary has three schoolbags.
Did she use her money well?

Anna's got a bank account.
She's got a bank account.
The bank gives Anna money.
Did she use her money well?

David bought a red bike.
He bought a red bike.
David can't ride a bike.
Did he use his money well?

## Unit 5 In space

We live on Earth.
It's a planet in space.
We go round the Sun.
We have our place.

Space is so big,
But you can see,
So many stars, so very far,
Through a telescope with me.

## Unit 6 Helping our planet

We need to use more solar power,
Do you know?
We need to use more water power,
Do you know?

We need to use more green power,
Wind, water, sun and heat underground.
We can help save our planet,
Do you know?

We need to use much less water,
Do you know?
We need to turn off lights at night,
Do you know?

We need to use more green power,
And less water, plastic and paper.
We can help save our planet,
Do you know? Do you know?
