## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Listen and number the pictures. Say more about these festivals.

（配图：节日图片：母亲节快乐、Carnival、Merry Christmas、Sunday, 22 June 赛龙舟）

**NOTICE**

Love boats? Like exciting races? The Dragon Boat Festival is coming. Come and join us!

Time: 8:30 a.m. Sunday
Place: Xingfu River
Xingfu Community

The notice about the dragon boat race finally came out last Friday. I was excited because my father was on a race team!

In the morning, my dad woke up early and went to the river. My mum and I went later and waited for the race. Then the judge counted down. The race began! I ran and cheered for my dad. His boat was very fast and his team won the race! They all sang their team song together. They were so happy!

In the evening, our family made zongzi and had a big dinner for the festival, and for the winner, my dad! It was the best Dragon Boat Festival ever!

Write F (Fact) or O (Opinion).

1. My father was on a race team. (　)
2. The judge counted down and the race began! (　)
3. His team won the race! (　)
4. It was the best Dragon Boat Festival ever! (　)

Read and answer.

1. What did the people in the text do on the Dragon Boat Festival?
2. What can you infer from "finally" in the first sentence?

### Reading time

（配图：戏剧场景插图，年兽与老人）

**The Monster Nian**

**Scene 1**

Narrator: One night, an old man came to a village. He saw the local people running away.
Old man: Excuse me, why are you running? What happened?
Villager 1: The monster Nian will come! Last year, it ate many animals. We are all afraid of it.
Old man: Wait! I have a good idea. I can help you!
Villager 2: The monster is very strong. You should run away to the mountains with us!
Old man: No. I will stay alone in the village and face Nian.

**Scene 2**

Narrator: The old man stayed in the village. Then, at midnight, the monster came.
Nian: What is that red paper on the doors and windows? I hate the colour red! Why are the windows so bright? I hate bright lights!
Old man: Aha! He looks worried.
Narrator: Then the old man set off fireworks. Bang! Bang! Bang!
Nian: Oh no! I hate loud noises! No, no!

**Scene 3**

Narrator: Nian was very afraid and ran back to the sea. The next day, the villagers came back to the village.
Villager 1: I can't believe it! All our animals are safe!
Villager 2: How did you do it?
Old man: Last night, I pasted red paper on the doors and windows and I also lit many candles. Finally, I set off fireworks. He ran away!
Villagers: Hooray! We are safe!
Narrator: From then on, on New Year's Eve, people get together to celebrate the coming new year. They paste *fu* and Spring Festival couplets on their doors and set off fireworks. This is why we also call the Spring Festival *guonian*.
