## Part B How can big events bring us together?

### Let's talk

Binbin, Chen Jie and Amy are talking about last weekend. Listen and answer.

1. What did Binbin do last weekend?
2. Did Robin go with Binbin?

Chen Jie: How was your weekend, Amy?
Amy: It was great. My mother and I went to a marathon on Sunday. She worked as a volunteer. I watched the race.
Chen Jie: What did you like about it?
Amy: I liked watching the runners. They ran very fast. I cheered for them.
Binbin: Nice! And you, Chen Jie? What did you do last weekend?
Chen Jie: I joined an online book fair. I liked it very much.
Binbin: What did you like about it?
Chen Jie: We shared ideas and talked about different books.
Binbin: That sounds fun!

- Did Amy and Chen Jie like the events?
- What did they like about them?

### Choose and discuss

（配图：词卡：birthday、sports meet、school trip）

- How was your ...?
- It was ...
- What did you like about it?

### Let's learn

（配图：五张大事件活动照片）

The writer read his book to the children.

People from different places ran a marathon.

Students made different robots.

Children sang and danced at an art festival.

Children wore beautiful clothes on China's National Day.

Zhang Peng: Did you go to the book fair yesterday?
Sarah: Yes, I did. I liked it.
Zhang Peng: What did you like about it?
Sarah: The writer read his book to us.
