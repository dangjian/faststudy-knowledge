Binbin: We ate some famous Xi'an noodles. Then we went to see the Terracotta Warriors.

## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Look at the text. Where could you find this? What does "Reviews" mean?

（配图：旅游网站页面，Flights / Hotels / Trains / Cars / Tours & Tickets / Attractions）

Jinggang Mountains　4.9/5 · 2,099 Reviews

No.1 Can't Miss Attraction in Jian　Mountain Climbing　Must-try Food

**Sun Lin　Xining, China**

A must-see　★★★★★

My trip to the Jinggangshan Revolution Museum was amazing! I walked around the museum for hours and saw many old books and photos. The volunteers told great stories about the Red Army. The stories were really inspiring. I learnt a lot. Do you want to learn more about the Red Army? You should go there!

**Colin　London, UK**

Wonderful views and food　★★★★★

This was a great place for a summer trip! We climbed the Jinggang Mountains and took many photos of the bamboo forest. We tried delicious pumpkin soup and red rice in a nice restaurant too. The Jinggang Mountains are only about 1.5 hours from the airport by bus. I want to go there again!

Read and answer.

1. What did you learn about the Jinggang Mountains?
2. What does the word "must-see" mean in the first review?
3. Do you want to go to the Jinggang Mountains after reading the reviews? Why?

### Reading time

（配图：Landmarks you shouldn't miss!，四篇游记）

**Eye-opening findings in Sanxingdui**

My trip to Sanxingdui was great! In the Sanxingdui Museum, I saw strange masks, the "big guy" statue and a life-size "fruit" tree. Some thought people from Sanxingdui were aliens. But it's not true. Maybe they ate hotpot three thousand years ago, just like we do today.
(Chen Li, history teacher)

**The pearl of Hangzhou**

I visited the West Lake last autumn. On the first night, my friends and I watched the moon rising from a boat on the lake. We ate mooncakes and drank Longjing tea. The next day we walked along the lake. It was such a nice trip.
(Jessica, nurse)

**A magical island**

On Easter Island, I saw huge statues called Moai. The Rapa Nui people made them hundreds of years ago. I loved the food on the island. The locals cooked fresh food on rocks! We had fish. It was so delicious!
(Lucy, 10-year-old student)

**An unusual "table"**

I climbed many mountains on my trips, but Table Mountain in South Africa was a special one. Its name comes from its top. It is flat like a table! But it only looks flat from one side. I went to the top by cable car. The first cable car opened in 1929. The view was so amazing!
(William, mountain lover)
