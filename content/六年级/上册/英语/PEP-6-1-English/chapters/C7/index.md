# Revision Learning in museums

## Read aloud and act it out

（配图：Miss White 与学生在教室讨论的插图）

Miss White: What do you want to learn about, children?
Binbin: I want to know about space and planets.
Chen Jie: I want to learn more about staying healthy.
Mike: I want to know more about Chinese festivals.
Miss White: Those are great ideas! What can help you learn about these things?
Chen Jie: We can read books or use the internet.
Mike: We can travel to different places.
Miss White: That's right. We can also learn in museums. This week, we're going to plan some museum trips.
Students: Great!

Miss White: Which museums do you want to visit? How much money do you need? Think about these questions and make a plan.

## Read and choose

Zhang Peng: That's a good idea!

Sarah: Ha! Ha! I want to see your photos.

A. I'm saving up for a new bike now.
B. I can watch videos and even buy nice gifts online.

D. I need a haircut.
E. Where are you going, Zhang Peng?

## Think and role-play

You are a school newspaper reporter. Ask Binbin and John some questions.

Reporter: What did you see in the museum?
Binbin: I saw a big spaceship. I also went into a mini space station. I ...
Reporter: Did you see any other interesting things there?
John: Yes. A robot dressed in a Tang suit. She pasted *fu* on the door.
Reporter: What did you like about the museum?
John: We watched a short play about Chang'e and Houyi. Then we saw another amazing robot. He gave us a speech.

## Read and give an ending to the play

（配图：嫦娥、玉兔与后羿在月宫的插图）

Narrator: It was the Mid-Autumn Festival. On the Moon, the Rabbit ran and jumped. Then he saw a big spaceship in the sky!

Houyi: Hello, Rabbit!
Rabbit: Hi, Houyi! Welcome!
Houyi: Thank you. How is Chang'e?
Rabbit: Chang'e is always sad, but she will be happy now. Look, here she comes. It's so good to have family around again!
Chang'e: How did you get to the Moon?
Houyi: A spaceship brought me here. We have great spaceships in China.
Chang'e: Wow! What did you do on Earth?
Houyi: Chang'e, you came to the Moon so long ago. What did you do here?
Chang'e: Rabbit and I read books and sang folk songs. But I always thought of our days together.

We live on ______. We are far from our neighbours, like the ______ and the ______. But with science and technology, we can find out more about them.

Smartphones and the internet bring us closer to each other. We can ______ our feelings with friends far away. Even in space, ______ can call their families.

Green energy like ______, wind or water power is also part of our life. We use technology to ______ energy use, ______ our waste and ______ resources.

## Read and take notes

（配图：博物馆"减压区"海报，Welcome!）

Did you enjoy listening to the bird song? Did you feel the cool wind from the sea? Is your stress gone?

Stress is sometimes bad for us. Science can help, but we also need to help ourselves. What can we do? Well, we should take care of our body. We need to have a healthy diet and exercise too. Remember, get enough sleep and don't stay up late.

We must also take care of our mind. Think about happy things. You can keep a diary, join some clubs, listen to music or read books. Spend time with your friends and family. Give them a big smile. Go out and have some fun!

I hope this place can help you!
