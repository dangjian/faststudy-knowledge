## Read and give an ending to the play

（配图：嫦娥、玉兔与后羿在月宫的插图）

Narrator: It was the Mid-Autumn Festival. On the Moon, the Rabbit ran and jumped. Then he saw a big spaceship in the sky!

Houyi: Hello, Rabbit!
Rabbit: Hi, Houyi! Welcome!
Houyi: Thank you. How is Chang'e?
Rabbit: Chang'e is always sad, but she will be happy now. Look, here she comes. It's so good to have family around again!
Chang'e: How did you get to the Moon?
Houyi: A spaceship brought me here. We have great spaceships in China.
Chang'e: Wow! What did you do on Earth?
Houyi: Chang'e, you came to the Moon so long ago. What did you do here?
Chang'e: Rabbit and I read books and sang folk songs. But I always thought of our days together.

## Read and take notes

（配图：博物馆"减压区"海报，Welcome!）

Did you enjoy listening to the bird song? Did you feel the cool wind from the sea? Is your stress gone?

Stress is sometimes bad for us. Science can help, but we also need to help ourselves. What can we do? Well, we should take care of our body. We need to have a healthy diet and exercise too. Remember, get enough sleep and don't stay up late.

We must also take care of our mind. Think about happy things. You can keep a diary, join some clubs, listen to music or read books. Spend time with your friends and family. Give them a big smile. Go out and have some fun!

I hope this place can help you!
