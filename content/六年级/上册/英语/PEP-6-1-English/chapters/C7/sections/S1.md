## Read aloud and act it out

（配图：Miss White 与学生在教室讨论的插图）

Miss White: What do you want to learn about, children?
Binbin: I want to know about space and planets.
Chen Jie: I want to learn more about staying healthy.
Mike: I want to know more about Chinese festivals.
Miss White: Those are great ideas! What can help you learn about these things?
Chen Jie: We can read books or use the internet.
Mike: We can travel to different places.
Miss White: That's right. We can also learn in museums. This week, we're going to plan some museum trips.
Students: Great!

Miss White: Which museums do you want to visit? How much money do you need? Think about these questions and make a plan.

## Read and choose

Sarah: Yes, I'm going to visit some museums online. ______
Zhang Peng: That's a good idea!
Sarah: ______
Zhang Peng: I'm going to visit the China Great Wall Museum.
Sarah: Wow! But it's very far. ______
Zhang Peng: I have 100 yuan. The museum is free. But I need to take a bus and buy some food and drink. Before that, ______ I want to look good in my photos.
Sarah: Ha! Ha! I want to see your photos.

A. I'm saving up for a new bike now.
B. I can watch videos and even buy nice gifts online.
C. How much are you going to take?
D. I need a haircut.
