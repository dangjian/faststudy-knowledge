## Unit 1

- How was your weekend?
  你周末过得怎么样？
- It was great! I climbed the Great Wall.
  非常棒！我去爬长城了。
- What did you do, Leo?
  你做什么了，利奥？
- I visited the "Gingerbread House" last Saturday.
  我上个星期六去参观“姜饼屋”了。
- Where did you go over the summer holidays, Binbin?
  你暑假去哪儿了，斌斌？
- I went to Xi'an with my family.
  我和家人去西安了。
- What did you do there?
  你们在那儿做了些什么？
- We went to see the Terracotta Warriors.
  我们去看兵马俑了。
- What else did you do?
  你们还做什么了？

## Unit 2

- How was your Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, Binbin?
  你中秋假期过得怎么样，斌斌？
- It was great!
  非常棒！
- Did you take a trip?
  你们去旅行了吗？
- No, we didn't.
  不，我们没有。
- Did you eat mooncakes?
  你们吃月饼了吗？
- Yes, we did.
  是的，我们吃了。
- My mother and I went to a marathon on Sunday.
  我和妈妈上周日去看了一场马拉松比赛。
- What did you like about it?
  你喜欢它哪一点？
- I liked watching the runners.
  我喜欢看参赛者（跑步）。
- What did you do last weekend?
  你上周末做什么了？

## Unit 3

- How do you feel?
  你感觉怎么样？
- I feel ill. My head hurts and I have a runny nose.
  我感觉不舒服。我头痛还流鼻涕。
- I'm sorry to hear that. Maybe you should see a doctor.
  对此我感到难过。也许你应该去看医生。
- How can we live a healthy life?
  我们怎样能过健康的生活？
- We need to eat healthy food. We should exercise too.
  我们需要吃健康的食物。我们还需要运动。
- Have a healthy diet.
  （保持）健康的饮食。
- Don't stay up late.
  不要熬夜。
- Think about happy things.
  想开心的事情。
- Join clubs and teams.
  加入俱乐部和团队。

## Unit 4

- How are you going to spend it?
  你打算怎么花（这些钱）？
- I'm going to buy a schoolbag.
  我打算买一个书包。
- I also want to buy some animal books.
  我还想买一些动物类书籍。
- What else are you going to buy?
  你还打算买什么？
- I'm going to buy a pair of sunglasses for Grandpa.
  我打算给爷爷买一副太阳镜。
- I also want to buy a microscope.
  我还想买一个显微镜。

## Unit 5

- What can you see in the sky?
  你能在天空中看到什么？
- We can see the Sun and clouds.
  我们能看到太阳和云。
- At night, we can see the Moon and the stars.
  在晚上，我们能看到月亮和星星。
- Is there any water on Mars?
  火星上有水吗？
- Yes, there is. There is soil too.
  是，有的。那里还有土壤。
- Are there any aliens?
  那儿有外星生物吗？
- I don't think so. They say there aren't any living things.
  我不这样认为。据说那里没有生物。
- China put a rover on Mars.
  中国在火星上放置了探测器。
- The taikonauts get up early in the morning.
  中国航天员很早就起床。

## Unit 6

- What do you know about it?
  关于它你知道些什么？
- Running water can make electricity.
  流水可以发电。
- What else do you want to know about water?
  关于水你还想了解些什么？
- Where does water come from?
  水从哪儿来？
- Wind power, solar power and water power are types of green energy.
  风能、太阳能和水能是不同类型的绿色能源。
- But many resources are running out.
  但是很多资源快要耗尽了。
- What should we do?
  我们该怎么办？
- How can we save energy?
  我们怎样才能节约能源？
- We can take quick showers.
  我们可以快速淋浴。
- We can also use LED lighting.
  我们还可以使用LED照明。
- We should use air conditioners less.
  我们应该少用空调。
