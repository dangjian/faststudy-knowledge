## Part B What do we know about Earth and its neighbours?

### Let's talk

Chen Jie is showing Miss White a magazine. Listen and choose.

1. What is the magazine about?
   A. Mars.　B. The Sun.　C. The Moon.
2. Why can't we live there?
   A. It is red.　B. It is very hot.　C. The air is thin.

Mike: Hey, Chen Jie. What are you reading about?
Chen Jie: Mars.
Oliver: Mars? What does it say?
Chen Jie: It talks about things on Mars.
Oliver: Wow! Is there any water on Mars?
Chen Jie: Yes, there is. There is soil too.
Oliver: Oh! Are there any aliens?
Chen Jie: I don't think so. They say there aren't any living things.
Oliver: I see.
Chen Jie: Look! This is the sunrise on Mars.
Mike: How beautiful! I really want to travel to Mars.
Chen Jie: Maybe you can go one day. China put a rover on Mars. We learn more about the "red planet" every year.

- What is on Mars?
- What else do you know about Mars?
