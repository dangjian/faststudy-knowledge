## Part C Read and write · Project · Reading time

### Read and write

Do you know any Chinese astronauts? List their names.

What do you know about an astronaut's life in space?

**What do astronauts do?**

Chinese astronauts are called taikonauts. Their life in space is special.

The taikonauts get up early in the morning. But getting ready in space is different. They can't use water to wash their faces. So taikonauts have to use a wet cloth. They brush their teeth with a special toothpaste and have to "eat" it!

Each day the taikonauts are very busy. They do a lot of work. For example, they grow vegetables and rice. Sometimes, they also give classes to children and answer questions about space.

It is hard work. The taikonauts must stay healthy. Every afternoon they do exercise to keep healthy and strong. But their job is also very interesting. They can see our beautiful Earth from space. The view is amazing! There is no other job like this one.

Read and answer.

1. What do taikonauts grow?
2. What do taikonauts do for children?
3. Why do taikonauts exercise?

### Reading time

（配图：太阳系主要成员示意图：Sun 太阳、Mercury 水星、Venus 金星、Earth 地球、Mars 火星、Jupiter 木星、Saturn 土星、Uranus 天王星、Neptune 海王星、Moon 月球）

**Some fun facts about our solar system**

The closest planet to the Sun is not the hottest. The hottest planet is Venus. It has a temperature (温度) of about 467°C.

The Great Red Spot (大红斑) is the name of a big storm on Jupiter. It is wider than Earth. It may be over 300 years old.

Neptune has the fastest wind in the solar system. Neptune's winds can reach over 2,000 kilometres per hour.

- Is there any water on these planets?
- Very little.
- Oh! So there are no fish.

Saturn has over two hundred moons. It may have more! Scientists are still looking for them.

- Are there any footprints on the Moon? How old are they?

Footprints on the Moon could stay there for a very long time. They could be there for thousands of years!

There are thousands of pieces of space junk (太空垃圾) floating around Earth. Space junk is man-made objects left in space. And the number is growing!

What other facts do you know?
