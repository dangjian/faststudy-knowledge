### What is fun on a farm?

#### 1a

#### 1b
Han Lin introduces his uncle's farm to Peter. Read the text and circle the things he talks about in the picture in 1a.

Look! This is my uncle's farm. It's a big farm. The grass is so green. You can see many kinds of animals. He has pigs, horses, cows, ducks, sheep, and chickens. How many ducks does my uncle have? Let me count. One, two, three, four, five, ... fourteen ducks! Oh, no. Look there! Another duck is behind the big tree. Do you see that house? That's my uncle's home. It's beautiful. Do you like my uncle's farm?
