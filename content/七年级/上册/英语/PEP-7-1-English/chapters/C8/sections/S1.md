# Unit 8 Fun Clubs

**UNIT 5**

**Big Question: Why do we join school clubs?**

**In this unit, you will**

1. talk about clubs at school.
2. talk about abilities using can.
3. find out what people do in different clubs.
4. discover your interests and talents.

**Look and share**

1. What are the students in the photo doing?
2. What club do you think they are in?
3. Do you want to join this club?
